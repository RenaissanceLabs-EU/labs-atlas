About

Hugging Face was founded in 2016 in New York by Clément Delangue, Julien Chaumond and Thomas Wolf. It began as a chatbot app for teenagers and shifted to machine learning tooling after the team open-sourced the model behind the app. Delangue is chief executive, Chaumond is chief technology officer and Wolf is chief science officer.

The company's open-source libraries, notably Transformers, became standard tools for working with pretrained models, and the Hub grew into the main place where researchers and companies publish models and datasets. In 2021 it launched the BigScience workshop, which produced the 176-billion-parameter multilingual BLOOM model in 2022. It also has a large presence in Paris and has expanded into robotics through the 2025 acquisition of Pollen Robotics.

Hugging Face earns revenue from hosted services and enterprise offerings while keeping its libraries and much of its research open. In September 2026 Nvidia announced an agreement to acquire the company, pending regulatory approval.

Mission and ambitions

Hugging Face states its aim as democratizing good machine learning by making models, datasets and tools open and shared by a community.
