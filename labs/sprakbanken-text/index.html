About

Språkbanken Text is a research and development unit at the University of Gothenburg, established in 1975 and among the world's oldest language technology units. It is now being renamed simply Språkbanken within a national infrastructure.

It builds research platforms including Korp (corpus search), Karp (lexicons), Mink, Sparv (text analysis) and Strix, and focuses on Swedish across historical periods. Lexical resources are released under CC-BY.

It takes part in CLARIN and HumInfra, and the national infrastructure is moving its news and data services to sprakbanken.se.

Mission and ambitions

The unit develops and shares language data and tools so researchers and society can work with Swedish text.
