About

Neuphonic is a text-to-speech company based in London. Its co-founders, who met studying machine learning at Cambridge, are Sohaib Ahmad as CEO and Jiameng Gao as CTO, according to press coverage.

It builds ultra-low-latency speech that can run locally, on premises or through an API, with multilingual voices and instant voice cloning. Its site lists the NeuTTS family and the NeuCodec audio codec and links to its GitHub and Hugging Face organizations.

The company has reported more than one million open-source downloads and says it is partnering with device makers.

Mission and ambitions

Neuphonic aims to make AI voice interaction immediate and natural through fast, private text-to-speech that developers can run anywhere.
