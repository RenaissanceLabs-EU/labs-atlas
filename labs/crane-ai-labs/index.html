About

Crane AI Labs is a Ugandan group co-founded by Kato Steven Mubiru and CTO Bakunga Bronson. Its Hugging Face organization hosts models, tokenizers and datasets for African languages.

In 2025 it released Ganda Gemma 1B for Luganda and Swahili Gemma 1B, with GGUF and LiteRT builds. In 2026 it added Luganda education assistants, MedGemma-based health models for offline use by rural health workers and ASR models.

Datasets such as UCCB, pedagogy benchmarks and a Luganda tokenizer evaluation support evaluation of low-resource languages.

Mission and ambitions

The group describes its aim as culturally relevant AI for the Global South, delivered on small models that can run on-device.
