About

Part AI publishes open Persian-language models on Hugging Face. Its Dorna family consists of decoder-only models trained or fine-tuned on Persian data, including Dorna2 variants built on Llama 3.1.

Its encoder line includes TookaBERT in base and large sizes, described in an arXiv paper as a step forward for Persian language understanding, and the Tooka-SBERT sentence embedding models in small and large sizes.

The models serve Persian NLP developers and researchers who need open baselines for a language that is underrepresented in multilingual models.

Mission and ambitions

Part AI works to strengthen Persian language technology by releasing models and benchmarks that others can build on.
