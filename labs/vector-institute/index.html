About

Vector opened in March 2017 at MaRS in Toronto as an independent nonprofit. Geoffrey Hinton became chief scientific adviser, Richard Zemel research director, and Garth Gibson CEO.

Its research is led by Hinton with research directors Daniel Roy and Graham Taylor as of 2023. Its GitHub organization hosts open engineering tools.

Mission and ambitions

Vector aims to drive excellence in machine learning and deep learning in Canada.
