About

Arc Institute was founded in 2021 by Silvana Konermann, Patrick Hsu and Patrick Collison with initial funding commitments of 650 million dollars. It gives researchers long-term support without grant applications.

In 2024 researchers led by Hsu and Brian Hie released Evo, a language model trained on biological sequence data, published in Science in November 2024. In April 2025 Arc announced the Virtual Cell Atlas with 10x Genomics and Ultima Genomics.

The institute publishes models and datasets on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Mission and ambitions

Arc aims to give scientists freedom to pursue long-horizon work on human disease, and increasingly applies machine learning to build virtual cell models.
