About

BigCode was established in September 2022 as an open scientific collaboration on the responsible development of code language models. It is stewarded by Hugging Face and ServiceNow and has drawn more than 1,100 members.

The Stack is a permissively licensed code dataset with an opt-out process for developers. StarCoder, trained on 1 trillion tokens, followed in May 2023 and StarCoder2 in early 2024. The project also maintains an evaluation harness.

Leandro von Werra of Hugging Face and Harm de Vries of ServiceNow Research co-lead the project. Because two companies host it, its independence is partial.

Mission and ambitions

BigCode aims to develop code language models openly and responsibly, with transparent data governance, community participation and reproducible evaluation.
