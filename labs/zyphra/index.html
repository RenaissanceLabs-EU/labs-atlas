About

Zyphra says it was founded in 2020 and lists members in Palo Alto, London and Montreal. Dealroom names Krithik Puthalath and Danny Martinelli as founders.

It has released ZAYA1-8B, a reasoning-focused mixture-of-experts model trained on AMD Instinct MI300 GPUs, and demonstrated large-scale training on a full AMD stack.

Mission and ambitions

Zyphra aims at full-stack, efficient AI models.
