About

TartuNLP is a natural language processing group at the University of Tartu in Estonia. Its site presents work on Estonian language models, machine translation and speech synthesis.

Named projects include Llammas, an Estonian-adapted Llama model, EstBERT, and the Neurotõlge translation service. Code and models are published through the TartuNLP GitHub organization.

Mission and ambitions

The group works to provide open language technology for Estonian and related smaller languages.
