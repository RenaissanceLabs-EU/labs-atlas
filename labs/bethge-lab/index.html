About

The Bethge Lab is led by Matthias Bethge, Professor of Computational Neuroscience and Machine Learning at the University of Tübingen and director of the Tübingen AI Center. Its GitHub organization, created in 2014, carries the tagline Perceiving Neural Networks.

The lab released Foolbox in 2017, a Python toolbox for benchmarking adversarial robustness under an MIT licence, and imagecorruptions and a detection robustness benchmark in 2019. It also published work on ImageNet-C robustness and on comparing machine and human vision.

Wieland Brendel, Jonas Rauber and Robert Geirhos appear among its public GitHub members and papers, and its code serves vision and robustness researchers.

Mission and ambitions

The lab seeks to understand how neural networks perceive images and to make them robust and more human-like, releasing benchmarks and tools openly.
