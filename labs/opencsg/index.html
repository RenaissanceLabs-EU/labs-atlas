About

OpenCSG describes itself as an open-source and fair initiative established in 2023 to build an open community for large language models. The name stands for Converged resources, Software refined and Generative LM.

Its main project is CSGHub, an Apache 2.0 platform for managing LLMs, datasets and agents with on-premise and hosted options. Other repositories cover the server, SDK, deployment charts and a lightweight local inference tool.

On Hugging Face it publishes datasets and models, with Agentic-27B and Agentic-30B-A3B among its recent releases.

Mission and ambitions

OpenCSG aims to help enterprises cut costs and raise efficiency in developing and deploying large language models through open tooling.
