About

Oumi emerged from stealth in January 2025 with a $10 million seed round and an open-source platform covering the model lifecycle, from data preparation and training to evaluation and deployment. It was built with researchers from more than a dozen universities.

The company is led by co-founder and CEO Manos Koukoumidis, a former Google Cloud engineering manager, with co-founder Oussama Elachqar and other founders who say they worked on PaLM at Google. Oumi PBC publishes its core library as open source and has released models such as the HallOumi hallucination detector.

Alongside the open library, Oumi offers a commercial platform with an agent that automates evaluation, data curation and training for teams building specialized models.

Mission and ambitions

Oumi wants AI development to be collective and reproducible, with open code, data and models rather than closed stacks. It describes its aim as giving AI a shared, Linux-like open foundation.
