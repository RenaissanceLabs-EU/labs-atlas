About

Tilde is a language technology company based in Latvia that builds machine translation and language tools. In 2024 it won the European Commission's Large AI Grand Challenge, which awarded 2 million GPU hours on the LUMI supercomputer.

On that compute it trained TildeOpen LLM, a 30B dense decoder-only model on the Llama 3 architecture, trained on 2 trillion tokens for 34 European languages. It was released in October 2025 on Hugging Face under CC BY 4.0.

A technical report presented at LREC 2026 describes the curriculum learning used to give lower-resource languages equitable representation.

Mission and ambitions

Tilde aims to give smaller European languages an open model that serves them as well as large ones.
