About

The Institute of Computer Science of the Polish Academy of Sciences in Warsaw hosts a Linguistic Engineering Group that builds resources for Polish. Its researchers co-trained HerBERT with Allegro, and Alina Wróblewska is a co-author of that work.

Its Hugging Face organization lists dozens of models and datasets, including the PolQA and MAUPQA question answering datasets, the NKJP1M corpus, the Silver Retriever retrieval model, and HerBERT variants for Silesian and Kashubian.

Mission and ambitions

To provide open language resources and models for Polish and related regional languages.
