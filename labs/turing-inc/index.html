About

Turing was founded in 2021 in Tokyo with the goal of fully autonomous vehicles by 2030. Issei Yamamoto is chief executive and Shunsuke Aoki co-founder and CTO.

Its Heron family of vision-language models, supported by Japan's GENIAC program, includes the Heron-NVILA-Lite 15B, 2B and 1B models. In March 2026 it reported Japan's first real-time public-road driving controlled by a vision-language-action model.

Mission and ambitions

Turing aims to deliver end-to-end AI driving for fully autonomous cars.
