About

Pleias is based at Station F in Paris and develops tools around training data: Common Corpus, an open collection of public-domain and permissively licensed text, Synth for synthetic training data, and Stratum for structuring documents.

Common Corpus draws on government records, legal archives, scientific literature and multilingual sources, and is available on Hugging Face. The team has also trained small models, including a 600-million-parameter model for the Paris transport operator RATP.

The company's authors include Pierre-Carl Langlais and Anastasia Stasenko, and it publishes research and models openly.

Mission and ambitions

Pleias argues that capable models can be built on data with clear provenance and licensing. Its goal is to make that approach practical for industry and public institutions.
