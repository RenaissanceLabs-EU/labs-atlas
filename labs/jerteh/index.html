About

The Society for Language Resources and Technologies, known as JeRTeh, is a Serbian association that runs seminars, publishes resources and takes part in projects such as UniDive, Distant Reading, WikiELTeC and It-Sr-NER. It maintains a GitHub organization opened in 2023.

It publishes Serbian models such as Jerteh-355 on Hugging Face and corpora including srpELTeC. In April 2026 it joined a protocol with the Office for IT and eGovernment and ten other institutions to build a national Serbian language model.

Mission and ambitions

To develop and share language resources and models for Serbian.

Milestones

  1. 2021

    BEaSTagger

    A stacked POS tagging system for Serbian was published.

  2. 2022

    It-Sr-NER

    CLARIN-compatible NER and geoparsing services for Italian and Serbian were released.

  3. 2023

    Jerteh-355 model

    A Serbian language model was published on Hugging Face.

  4. 2024

    NovaIskra project

    A cultural heritage project with UNILIB was started.

  5. 2026

    National language model protocol

    JeRTeh and eleven institutions signed a protocol to build a Serbian national model.
