About

HiTZ is the Basque Center for Language Technology at the University of the Basque Country. It develops language technology for Basque and other languages, and its Hugging Face and GitHub organisations publish models and tools.

Its releases include Basque and multilingual language models and information-extraction tools. Detailed history and staff could not be verified because the center's site blocked automated access.

Mission and ambitions

HiTZ works to give Basque and other under-resourced languages modern language technology, openly shared.

Milestones

  1. 2020

    HiTZ research center presented

    The Basque Government presented HiTZ as a research center grouping the Ixa and Aholab groups of the University of the Basque Country.

  2. 2023

    Basque Government funding

    The Basque Government announced 1.68 million euros for the center's language-technology research.

  3. 2024

    Latxa

    Released a family of 7B to 70B Basque language models built on Llama 2, with a new Basque corpus and evaluation suite, under the IKER-GAITU project.

  4. 2025

    Latxa Instruct on Llama 3.1

    Published instruction-tuned Latxa models for Basque at 8B and 70B and a public chatbot, with a crowdsourced arena collecting over 12,000 preference judgments.
