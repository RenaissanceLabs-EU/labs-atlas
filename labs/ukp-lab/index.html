About

The Ubiquitous Knowledge Processing Lab belongs to the Department of Computer Science at TU Darmstadt and was founded by Iryna Gurevych. Its early work covered information retrieval, question answering and the structured use of wikis, including the Java Wikipedia Library and Java Wiktionary Library.

It created Sentence-BERT, the basis of the Sentence Transformers library, with Nils Reimers in 2019, and develops AdapterHub. In October 2025 maintenance of Sentence Transformers passed to Hugging Face under Apache 2.0.

Mission and ambitions

The lab works on open, reusable language technology and knowledge processing methods.
