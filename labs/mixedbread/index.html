About

Mixedbread is an applied research lab focused on retrieval. It builds embedding, reranking and search systems that give AI applications the right context at the right time.

The team combines deep learning research with high-performance systems engineering and offers its models through a hosted search product while publishing open-source models on Hugging Face. The company states that those open models have been downloaded more than 50 million times.

Recent work covers listwise rerankers, quantization for late-interaction retrieval and ingestion of very large files.

Mission and ambitions

Mixedbread aims to build memory for AI, so that intelligent systems have the context they need to understand and act on the world.
