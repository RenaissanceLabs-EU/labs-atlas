About

Arcee AI was founded in 2023 by Mark McQuade, Jacob Solawetz and Brian Benedict to make smaller, specialized language models practical for enterprises. It began by helping teams adapt open models and later moved to training its own models in San Francisco.

Its open tooling includes MergeKit for merging pretrained models, described in a 2024 paper, and DistillKit for distillation. Its current offerings include the Trinity model family, an open source catalog and an API platform.

Investors named on its site include Samsung, M12, Hitachi Ventures and Flybridge. Reporting indicates a 24 million dollar Series A in 2024.

Mission and ambitions

Arcee states that the future of AI should be something people can build on, adapt and own.
