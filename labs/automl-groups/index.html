About

The AutoML groups at the University of Freiburg and Leibniz University Hannover were led by Frank Hutter and Marius Lindauer, with Matthias Feurer and Katharina Eggensperger among close collaborators. The GitHub organization dates from 2014.

Their system auto-sklearn began as a competition entry and won the first international AutoML challenge in 2015 to 2016 and the second in 2017 to 2018. The 2015 NeurIPS paper introduced it, and auto-sklearn 2.0 appeared in JMLR in 2022. SMAC3 and Auto-PyTorch extend the work to Bayesian optimisation and deep learning.

In an August 2026 post the group announced the closure of automl.org after a decade. Hutter now leads Prior Labs and Lindauer's Hannover group has broadened beyond AutoML.

Mission and ambitions

The groups aimed to make machine learning accessible by automating model and hyperparameter selection, and released their tools as open source for the community.

  1. 2015

  2. 2016

    First AutoML challenge win

    The team won the first international AutoML challenge.

  3. 2018

    Second AutoML challenge win

    PoSH-Auto-sklearn won the second challenge against 41 other systems.

  4. 2020

    Auto-PyTorch

    Released automated deep learning tooling (arXiv 2006.13799).

  5. 2021

    SMAC3

    Published a versatile Bayesian optimisation package (arXiv 2109.09831).

  6. 2022

    auto-sklearn 2.0

    The JMLR paper reported stronger results in 10 minutes than version 1.0 in 60.
