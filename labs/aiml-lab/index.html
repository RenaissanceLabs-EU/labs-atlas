About

The AIML Lab at TU Darmstadt is led by Professor Kristian Kersting, who joined the university in 2019 and also co-directs hessian.AI. Its stated aims are advancing statistical learning, probabilistic reasoning and collaborative intelligence, with work in statistical relational AI, generative modeling and trustworthy machine learning.

The lab publishes code on GitHub and models on Hugging Face, where it lists 20 models and 42 datasets. Safety tools include Safe Latent Diffusion, LlavaGuard and QwenGuard, and recent releases cover OlmoLogic reasoning models and the KletterMix German pretraining data.

Its audience is researchers and developers who need open checks for image-text datasets and generative models.

Mission and ambitions

The lab aims to make machines learn about the world rapidly and flexibly, combining logical and probabilistic methods with deep learning. It emphasises trustworthy and safe generative systems.
