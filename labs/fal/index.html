About

ÚFAL was established in 1990 at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, continuing the Laboratory of Algebraic Linguistics active since the early 1960s.

It develops UDPipe, a trainable pipeline for tokenization, tagging, lemmatization and parsing under MPL 2.0, and the Prague Dependency Treebank family.

Mission and ambitions

The institute advances computational linguistics and language resources.
