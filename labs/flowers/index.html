About

The Flowers team investigates the mechanisms of sensorimotor, cognitive and social development in humans and machines. It is housed at Inria Bordeaux-Sud-Ouest and Univ. Bordeaux and is headed by Pierre-Yves Oudeyer, a research director known for work on artificial curiosity and intrinsic motivation.

The team's GitHub organization, opened in 2012, publishes research code such as the Explauto exploration library and testbeds for automatic curriculum learning in deep reinforcement learning. More recent releases ground language models in interactive environments using online reinforcement learning and provide the Lamorel library for that purpose.

Mission and ambitions

To understand how open-ended, curiosity-driven learning works and to build artificial agents that develop and learn in similar ways.
