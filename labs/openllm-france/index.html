About

OpenLLM France grew out of a community of academic and industry stakeholders interested in open-source generative models, with a GitHub organization created in June 2023. Its current research project is funded by BPI France for two years and run by a consortium of nine official partners with 12 associate partners.

Lucie-7B, published in October 2024 under Apache 2.0, was the first foundation model the project trained from scratch, with more than 30 percent French training data. The newer Luciole family covers 1B, 8B and 23B models with about 30 percent French data, with training data and code released on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Public GitHub members include researchers affiliated with Linagora and CNRS.

Mission and ambitions

The project aims to build open digital commons for the French language and share expertise for ethical, open AI applications in France.
