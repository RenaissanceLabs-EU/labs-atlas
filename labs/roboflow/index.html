About

Roboflow was started by Joseph Nelson (CEO) and Brad Dwyer (CTO) after their 2017 Magic Sudoku augmented reality app. The company's own site says they launched it in 2020, while other sources date incorporation to 2019.

Open-source projects include Supervision for vision utilities, Inference for running models, and RF-DETR, a real-time detector accepted at ICLR 2026. Roboflow Universe hosts shared datasets and models.

RF-DETR authors include Peter Robicheaux, James Gallagher and Isaac Robinson alongside Nelson.

Mission and ambitions

Roboflow wants to make computer vision accessible to every developer.
