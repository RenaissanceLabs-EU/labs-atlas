About

SpeakLeash, also known as Spichlerz, is an open community project founded in 2022 that collects Polish text for language modeling. Sebastian Kondracki is described as co-creator.

The Bielik family of Polish models, from 1.5B to 11B parameters, was developed with ACK Cyfronet AGH, which provides the Helios supercomputer. Bielik 11B v2 has a technical report on arXiv, and Bielik 11B v3 followed in 2025 under Apache 2.0, with authors including Krzysztof Ociepa, Łukasz Flis, Remigiusz Kinas, Krzysztof Wróbel and Adrian Gwoździej.

Models and datasets are published on Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

SpeakLeash aims to give Polish a strong open language model ecosystem.
