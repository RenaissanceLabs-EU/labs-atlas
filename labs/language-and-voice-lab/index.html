About

The Language and Voice Lab develops speech technology for Icelandic. It ran the crowd-sourced Samrómur corpus with Almannarómur, the Icelandic Center for Language Technology, and the latest release holds about 2,233 hours of recordings.

It also released the Althingi parliamentary corpus of about 542 hours covering 2005 to 2016, the first public Icelandic corpus of children's voices, and synthetic data generated with its TTS systems. The datasets and models are licensed openly on Hugging Face, OpenSLR and LDC.

Mission and ambitions

To make Icelandic speech technology openly available, with large corpora and models for recognition and synthesis.
