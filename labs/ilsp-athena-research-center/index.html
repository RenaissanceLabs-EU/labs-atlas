About

The Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) is part of Athena Research Center in Athens and works on language and speech technology. It has recently marked 35 years of activity. Its research covers language resources, speech processing and, more recently, large language models for Greek.

In March 2024 the institute released Meltemi 7B, trained on a corpus of about 40 billion Greek tokens, and described as the first open large language model for Greek in a July 2024 paper. It followed with Llama-Krikri-8B, built on Llama 3.1 with an extended Greek tokenizer and a 128k context window, trained on about 56.7 billion Greek tokens and supporting polytonic and Ancient Greek.

Models and instruction-tuned variants are published on Hugging Face under the ilsp organization, giving Greek developers and researchers open alternatives to general multilingual models.

Mission and ambitions

ILSP develops language and speech technology for Greek and openly releases the resulting models and resources so that the language is well supported in modern AI systems.
