About

The Maltese Language Resource Server is a University of Malta project that produces tools and resources for Maltese, English and Maltese Sign Language. It is coordinated by the Institute of Linguistics and Language Technology together with the Department of Artificial Intelligence.

The server hosts Korpus Malti, an annotated corpus of roughly 250 million tokens in its version 3.0, as well as a learner corpus, an open lexicon and a sign language dictionary. A 2016 electronic Maltese dictionary built on the group's lexicon was launched with the Malta Communications Authority.

More recently the group has released Maltese language models and datasets on Hugging Face under the MLRS organisation, for researchers and developers working on Maltese.

Mission and ambitions

The project aims to supply the open language data and software that Maltese needs to be supported by modern language technology.

Milestones

  1. 2006

    MLRS project described in print

    An early paper describes the Maltese Language Resource Server as a two-year research project at the University of Malta.

  2. 2016

    Korpus Malti v3.0 tagged

    Version 3.0 of the corpus was annotated with an SVMTool tagger reporting about 97 percent accuracy.

  3. 2016

    Official online Maltese dictionary launched

    A dictionary built on the group's lexicon was launched with the Malta Communications Authority in March.

  4. 2022

    Maltese BERT models released

    The group published new corpora and BERT-style models for Maltese on Hugging Face.
