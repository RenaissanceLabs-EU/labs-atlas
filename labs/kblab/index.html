About

KBLab is a research department within the National Library of Sweden, set up in 2019 as a national research infrastructure for digital humanities and social science. It gives researchers access to the library's collections in structured form. The lab is directed by Love Börjeson.

Using the legal-deposit and digitized holdings, KBLab trains Swedish models. Its 2020 paper by Martin Malmsten, Love Börjeson and Chris Haffenden introduced KB-BERT. The lab later released KB-Whisper, speech recognition models trained on more than 50,000 hours of Swedish speech, which it reports outperform OpenAI's Whisper at each size on Swedish.

Models are published on Hugging Face, with the large KB-Whisper model under the Apache 2.0 license.

Mission and ambitions

KBLab aims to make the National Library's collections usable for research through language and speech models and open tools, supporting digital humanities and social science.
