About

INSAIT, the Institute for Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Technology, was created by decree of the Bulgarian Council of Ministers in February 2021 and began operations in April 2022. It is hosted at Sofia University and set up in partnership with ETH Zurich, EPFL and the Government of Bulgaria. Its founder and scientific director is Martin Vechev, a professor at ETH Zurich.

The Bulgarian government committed about 85 million euros over ten years, and donors include Google, AWS, DeepMind, SiteGround and VMware. Besides foundation models, the institute runs research in computer vision, robotics, security and formal methods, and spins out companies such as LogicStar AI.

Its open model line includes BgGPT for Bulgarian and MamayLM for Ukrainian, which was developed with ETH Zurich on Gemma 2 9B. In 2024 it also released COMPL-AI, a framework for evaluating models against the EU AI Act.

Mission and ambitions

INSAIT states its mission as achieving excellence in science, research and education, and it applies this to building language technology for under-served European languages.
