About

Giskard describes its mission as securing conversational AI, with a platform for red teaming, LLM evaluation and vulnerability scanning. Alex Combessie and Jean-Marie John-Mathews are co-founders and co-CEOs.

The company maintains the open-source Giskard library and publishes the Phare benchmark. Its partners include the European Commission, Bpifrance, Hugging Face and Mistral AI, and investors include Julien Chaumond and Elaia.

Mission and ambitions

Giskard aims to make AI agents and language models safe and reliable through systematic testing.
