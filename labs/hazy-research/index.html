About

Hazy Research is a Stanford computer science group led by Professor Chris Re. It studies learning from weak supervision and the mathematical foundations of these methods, and turns the results into systems that help people build and maintain machine learning models.

Its blog covers GPU kernel programming with ThunderKittens, HipKittens and ParallelKittens, efficient architectures including Monarch Mixer, Based, Hyena, S4 and FlashFFTConv, local on-device models such as Minions and OpenJarvis, and long-context memory techniques such as Cartridges.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to make machine learning systems faster and easier to build by pairing theory with open implementations.
