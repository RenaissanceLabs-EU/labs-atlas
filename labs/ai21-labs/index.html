About

AI21 Labs was founded in Tel Aviv in November 2017 by Yoav Shoham, Ori Goshen and Amnon Shashua. Shoham and Goshen serve as co-CEOs and Shashua is chairman.

The company launched the Jurassic-1 language models in August 2021 and released the first Jamba model in March 2024. Jamba 1.5 followed in 2024 and Jamba 1.6 in March 2025, and its Hugging Face organisation now lists Jamba2 and Jamba Reasoning models.

Its main business is commercial AI for enterprises, with the open Jamba weights published alongside paid products.

Mission and ambitions

AI21 describes its aim as building reliable, practical and scalable AI for the enterprise.
