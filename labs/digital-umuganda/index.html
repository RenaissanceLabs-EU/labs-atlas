About

Digital Umuganda is a Rwanda-based African startup whose stated goal is to create and promote voice infrastructure for African languages. Its interests are African natural language processing, speech-to-text, text-to-speech and data collection.

Its Hugging Face organisation lists 12 members, 39 models and 22 datasets. Releases include Kinyarwanda and Swahili speech recognition models, a Kinyarwanda translation model, a text-to-speech model and the AfriVoice speech datasets.

Mission and ambitions

Digital Umuganda aims to build the speech and language resources that African languages need to be supported by modern AI.

Milestones

  1. 2019

    Kinyarwanda voice hackathon

    A 2019 Mozilla and GIZ hackathon in Kigali launched the Kinyarwanda voice data effort; the winning idea adapted the umuganda community-work tradition to collect speech.

  2. 2019-2022

    Common Voice contributions

    The company became a leading African contributor to Mozilla Common Voice, building open Kinyarwanda speech data and models.
