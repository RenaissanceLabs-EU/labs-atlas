About

TurkuNLP is a group of about 30 researchers at the University of Turku, ranging from research assistants to professors. Its main topics are syntactic and semantic analysis of Finnish, large language models for Finnish and other languages, biomedical and clinical text mining, and the analysis of internet registers and genres.

The group's resources include FinBERT, WikiBERT, FinGPT and the Turku Dependency Treebank. With Silo AI's SiloGen it built the Poro and Viking models on the LUMI supercomputer; Viking, released in 2024 in 7B, 13B and 33B sizes, covers the Nordic languages, English and code under Apache 2.0.

TurkuNLP co-hosts an open monthly seminar on NLP and AI with Omni Language AI Research, and publishes its code and models on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to produce open language resources and models for Finnish and the Nordic languages.
