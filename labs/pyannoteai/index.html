About

pyannote.audio was created by Hervé Bredin and collaborators, described in an ICASSP 2020 paper, and is released under the MIT licence. Its pretrained models are widely downloaded on Hugging Face.

pyannoteAI was founded in 2024 with Vincent Molina as CEO and Bredin as chief science officer. It sells faster hosted models such as precision-2.

In late 2025 the project released pyannote.audio 4.0 with the open community-1 model.

Mission and ambitions

The team aims to give developers accurate speaker intelligence, keeping the core toolkit open while funding development through hosted models.
