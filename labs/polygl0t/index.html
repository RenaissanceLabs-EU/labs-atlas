About

Polygl0t, also referred to as Project Polyglot, develops efficient language models for low-resource languages. It is funded by the German BMBF and the North Rhine-Westphalia ministry as part of TRA Sustainable Futures at the University of Bonn, and trains on Bonn's Marvin cluster and Jülich's JUPITER Booster.

Its Tucano models, first described in 2024, were trained on GigaVerbo, a corpus of about 200 billion deduplicated Portuguese tokens. The follow-up Tucano 2 paper of March 2026 introduces GigaVerbo-v2, with about 320 billion tokens, and models from roughly 0.5B to 3.7B parameters.

The group publishes weights, datasets and training code, including an LLM foundry repository and a fork of the evaluation harness.

Mission and ambitions

Polygl0t aims to close the linguistic divide in NLP by making capable open models for languages that large developers serve poorly.
