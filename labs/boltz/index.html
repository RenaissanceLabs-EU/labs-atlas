About

Boltz PBC builds AI models and infrastructure for biomolecular design and drug discovery. Its platform, Boltz Lab, lets scientists design small molecules and biologics.

Its open-source models include Boltz-2 and BoltzGen, while BoltzProt-1 and BoltzMol-1 are proprietary. The site reports more than 5 million downloads and over 1,000 companies using its tools, and announced an agreement with Bristol Myers Squibb in October 2026.

Mission and ambitions

Boltz aims to make biomolecular modelling broadly available while building a commercial platform on top.
