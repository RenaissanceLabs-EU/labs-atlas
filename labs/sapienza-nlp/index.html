About

Sapienza NLP is headed by Roberto Navigli, a professor at Sapienza and Fellow of AAAI, ACL, EurAI and ELLIS. His ERC grants (MultiJEDI and MOUSSE) funded work on BabelNet and word sense disambiguation.

In April 2024 the group released Minerva, a family of language models trained from scratch on Italian and English, each on more than 500 billion words. The work was led by Navigli with Edoardo Barba and Simone Conia within the FAIR project, with CINECA compute.

The group publishes tools for entity linking, coreference and semantics.

Mission and ambitions

The group studies multilingual meaning and builds open resources for Italian and other languages.
