About

VITA, the Visual Intelligence for Transportation lab, is led by Alexandre Alahi at EPFL, where he joined in 2017. It works on socially aware AI for safe autonomous systems and mobility.

Its open code includes OpenPifPaf for human pose estimation, which was combined with commercial licensing for technology transfer, along with trajectory prediction and video generation projects on GitHub.

Mission and ambitions

The lab makes AI socially aware for transportation.
