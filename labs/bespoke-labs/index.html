About

Bespoke Labs is a data research lab in the SF Bay Area. Its focus is RL environments, agent evaluation and optimisation, and benchmarking for production agents.

It maintains Curator, an open library for data curation, and contributes to Terminal-Bench and GEPA. Its site lists OpenThoughts, an open reasoning dataset presented at ICLR 2026.

The OpenThoughts collaboration, led by researchers across Stanford, UC Berkeley, UT Austin, LAION and other institutions, launched in January 2025 and released OpenThoughts3 in June 2025.

Mission and ambitions

Bespoke aims to give frontier labs and enterprises the data and environments needed to train long-horizon agents.
