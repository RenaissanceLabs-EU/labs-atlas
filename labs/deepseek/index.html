About

DeepSeek was founded on 17 July 2023 as a spin-off from the AI research lab of High-Flyer, a Chinese hedge fund that owns and backs it. Liang Wenfeng founded the company and serves as CEO, and it had about 160 employees in 2025.

The lab released DeepSeek-V2 in May 2024, which introduced multi-head latent attention within a mixture-of-experts design, and DeepSeek-V3 in December 2024. DeepSeek-R1 followed on 20 January 2025 alongside a chatbot, with weights under the MIT License. Later releases include V3.1 in August 2025, V3.2 in December 2025 and a V4 preview in April 2026.

Wikipedia reports that DeepSeek closed a $7 billion Series A at a $52 billion post-money valuation in May 2026, ending its reliance on its parent alone. The company publishes weights and technical reports but not training data, and several governments have restricted its use on official systems.

Mission and ambitions

DeepSeek pursues capable general models and publishes weights and technical reports for them, funded originally by High-Flyer and, from 2026, by outside investors as well.
