About

rinna was established in 2020 when the team behind Microsoft's Japanese Rinna chatbot, launched in 2015, separated from Microsoft to operate independently.

Its research group releases base models for Japanese text, audio and images, including GPT, BERT, HuBERT, CLIP and Stable Diffusion variants, on Hugging Face and GitHub. Its early Japanese GPT-2 models became common baselines for Japanese NLP.

The Youri series continues pretraining of Llama 2 on about 40 billion tokens of mixed Japanese and English data, and the Nekomata series builds on Alibaba's Qwen models with a tokenizer that is more efficient for Japanese.

Mission and ambitions

rinna aims to give Japanese developers open pretrained models for their language.
