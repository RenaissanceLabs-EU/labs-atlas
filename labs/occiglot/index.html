About

Occiglot describes itself as an ongoing research collective and an informal community of motivated individuals building open multilingual language models for Europe. Its announcement in March 2024 framed the work as preserving linguistic diversity and AI sovereignty in Europe.

The group released continually pre-trained Occiglot-7B models for German, French, Italian, Spanish and combined European language sets in March 2024, followed by the Occiglot-Fineweb dataset and German models built with DiscoResearch. It also published a tokenizer evaluation for European languages and contributed to the Community OSCAR web corpus.

The page names contributors Manuel Brack, Malte Ostendorff and Pedro Ortiz Suarez, and invites collaborators through its Discord server.

Mission and ambitions

Occiglot works to build transparent, open-source multilingual language models for European languages through an open community.

Milestones

  1. 2024

    Occiglot announcement

    The collective published its mission post on 6 March 2024.

  2. 2024

    Occiglot-7B model family

    Language-specific and eu5 Occiglot-7B models appeared on Hugging Face in early March 2024.

  3. 2024

    Tokenizer evaluation

    An analysis of tokenizers on European languages published on 11 March 2024.

  4. 2024

    Occiglot-Fineweb

    A preliminary dataset of about 230 million cleaned documents in 10 languages.

  5. 2024

    German Llama-3 models

    Base, instruct and long-context German models built with DiscoResearch on 65 billion German tokens.

  6. 2024

    Community OSCAR

    A multilingual web dataset built from 40 monthly Common Crawl snapshots, announced in August 2024.
