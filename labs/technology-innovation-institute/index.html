About

The Technology Innovation Institute is part of the Advanced Technology Research Council of the Abu Dhabi government. It works across AI, quantum computing, robotics, cryptography and other fields, and its Artificial Intelligence and Digital Science Research Center builds the Falcon models.

Falcon 40B and 7B were released in 2023, followed by Falcon 180B in September 2023, Falcon 2 11B in May 2024 and Falcon 3 in December 2024. Later work includes the hybrid Falcon-H1 family and Falcon-H1 Arabic in January 2026.

TII is state funded, so its openness follows its license choices per release.

Mission and ambitions

TII aims to build sovereign, efficient AI models and release them for the global research community.
