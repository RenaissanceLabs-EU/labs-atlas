About

Physical Intelligence builds general-purpose foundation models for robots rather than robot hardware. The company was co-founded by Karol Hausman, Sergey Levine, Chelsea Finn and others, and draws on research from Google, UC Berkeley and Stanford.

Its pi0 model combines a vision-language backbone with a flow-matching action expert to generate robot actions. The company released pi0 weights and training and inference code through the openpi repository in early 2025.

Researchers and robotics developers use openpi to fine-tune policies on their own robots, which makes the project one of the main open entry points to robot foundation models.

Mission and ambitions

The company aims to build a general-purpose model that can control many kinds of robots across many tasks. It shares part of its research openly to help the field progress.
