About

Miðeind was founded in 2015 by Vilhjálmur Þorsteinsson, a long-time software entrepreneur. It develops language technology and AI for Icelandic, a language whose small market, limited data and complex grammar leave it poorly served by large technology firms.

Its products include Málfríður for spelling and grammar correction, Hreimur for speech recognition, Erlendur for translation, Snara for dictionary search and Lísa for text extraction, all gathered on the Málstaður site. The company states that its software is mostly open source.

Miðeind was an implementation partner in the Icelandic government's Language Technology Programme from 2019 to 2022 and maintains a Hugging Face leaderboard that evaluates large language models on Icelandic.

Mission and ambitions

Miðeind works to strengthen Icelandic in AI and language technology, doing so as openly as possible so that the public, businesses and researchers all benefit.

  2. 2019
