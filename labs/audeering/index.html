About

audEERING was founded in 2012 as a spin-off of Technical University of Munich research, with Dagmar Schuller, Florian Eyben, Björn Schuller and Martin Wöllmer named as founders in a 2012 report. It is based in Gilching near Munich.

openSMILE began in 2008 at TUM within the EU SEMAINE project. In 2013 audEERING obtained the rights and published version 2.0 under a research licence. The toolkit is free for research and personal use, while commercial users need a licence, so it is source-available rather than fully open.

The company also maintains open repositories on audio machine learning, including the w2v2-how-to guide and wav2vec2-based emotion and age-gender models on Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

audEERING develops audio analysis and speech emotion technology and shares research tooling with the scientific community while selling commercial products.
