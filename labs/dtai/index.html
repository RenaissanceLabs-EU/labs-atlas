About

DTAI is the Declarative Languages and Artificial Intelligence research group in the Department of Computer Science at KU Leuven in Belgium. Its academic staff includes Hendrik Blockeel, Jesse Davis, Luc De Raedt, Tias Guns and Tom Schrijvers, and it is linked to the ADVISE, EAVISE and M-Group research groups.

In January 2020 researchers Pieter Delobelle, Thomas Winters and Bettina Berendt introduced RobBERT, a Dutch RoBERTa-based language model. The group has since refreshed it with RobBERT-2022 and RobBERT-2023, which are published under the DTAI-KULeuven organization on Hugging Face under the MIT license.

Mission and ambitions

To advance machine learning and reasoning methods and to supply open language resources for Dutch.

Milestones

  1. 2020

    RobBERT released

    A Dutch RoBERTa-based language model was introduced in a paper by Delobelle, Winters and Berendt.

  2. 2022

    RobBERT-2022

    An update with an extended tokenizer and further pretraining on recent Dutch OSCAR data to reflect evolving language use.

  3. 2023

    RobBERT-2023 models published

    A Dutch base model initialized from RoBERTa weights and a first 355M-parameter large Dutch variant were released under the MIT license.

  4. 2024

    RobBERT-2023 journal paper

    Delobelle and Remy published the model conversion method in the Computational Linguistics in the Netherlands Journal.
