About

MiniMax was founded in December 2021 in Shanghai by Yan Junjie, Yun Yeyi and Zhou Yucong, who came from computer vision research at SenseTime. It began research in January 2022 and released its first text model, abab1, in April 2022. The company listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in January 2026.

It develops general-purpose models across text, speech, music, image and video, and serves consumer products such as the Hailuo video service and the Talkie character app alongside an API platform. Its company page reports more than 300 million users in over 230 countries and regions.

The MiniMax-M series of language models is published on Hugging Face under the MiniMaxAI organization, with recent releases including MiniMax-M3, M3.1-Flash and the H3 image models. MiniMax is a commercial, publicly listed company that releases some weights openly.

Mission and ambitions

MiniMax describes its mission as Intelligence with Everyone, building its own foundation models toward artificial general intelligence.
