About

Uned Technolegau Iaith is a self-funded research unit at Bangor University that develops digital language resources for Welsh, other Celtic languages and multilingual settings. Delyth Prys is head of the unit, and Gruffudd Prys, Dewi Bryn Jones and colleagues author its work.

It runs the National Language Technologies Portal and maintains Welsh speech recognition, text-to-speech, translation and terminology tools. Its public repositories date back to 2014, and it has worked with Welsh Common Voice, the Welsh government and S4C.

Mission and ambitions

To make language technology available for Welsh and to share the tools openly.

Milestones

  1. 2014

    GitHub organization opened

    The unit began publishing code on GitHub.

  2. 2015

    MosesSMT Welsh translation

    Docker tooling for Welsh statistical machine translation was published.

  3. 2016

    New resources for Welsh

    The unit released resources for communicating with computers in Welsh, funded by the Welsh government and S4C.

  4. 2018

    DeepSpeech Welsh recipes

    Docker tooling for training Welsh speech recognition was released.

  5. 2021

    HuggingFace Welsh STT

    Tooling for Welsh speech recognition with Hugging Face was released.
