About

NovaSky is a team within the Sky Computing Lab at UC Berkeley. Its GitHub organization was created in January 2025, the month it published Sky-T1, a reasoning model it said could be trained for under 450 dollars.

The group releases weights, data and training code together. After Sky-T1 it published work on cutting reasoning costs, test-time scaling for code generation and the SkyRL framework, which grew from SkyRL-v0 for long-horizon software agents into a modular RL library for training LLMs.

Its repositories, including SkyThought and SkyRL, are released under Apache 2.0, and its audience is researchers and developers training reasoning and agentic models.

Mission and ambitions

NovaSky aims to make post-training of reasoning and agentic models reproducible and affordable by sharing models, data and code openly.

Milestones

  1. 2025

    Sky-T1-32B-Preview

    An open 32-billion-parameter reasoning model, with data and code, reported to cost under 450 dollars to train.

  2. 2025

    Reasoning cost reduction

    A January 2025 post showed how to cut reasoning costs by about half without sacrificing accuracy.

  3. 2025

    SkyRL-v0

    A May 2025 release for training long-horizon real-world agents with reinforcement learning.

  4. 2025

    SkyRL-SQL

    A May 2025 recipe for data-efficient multi-turn reinforcement learning on text-to-SQL.

  5. 2025

    SkyRL-v0.1

    A June 2025 release evolving SkyRL into a modular, performant RL framework for LLMs.

  6. 2025

    SA-SWE-32B

    A 32-billion-parameter software-engineering model published in October 2025.
