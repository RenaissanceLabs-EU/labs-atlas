About

The Music Technology Group is a research group of the Department of Information and Communication Technologies at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona. It was founded in 1994 by Xavier Serra, who still directs it.

Its research covers audio signal processing, sound and music description, musical interaction, and sound and music communities. The group releases much of its software as open source on GitHub.

Its best known software is Essentia, a C++ library with Python bindings for audio analysis and music information retrieval. The library began in 2006 to replace a set of ad hoc C++ programs and is released under the AGPLv3 licence.

Mission and ambitions

The group works to advance sound and music computing and to turn that research into open tools used by scientists, developers and artists.
