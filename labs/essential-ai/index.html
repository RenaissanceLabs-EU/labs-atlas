About

Essential AI is led by CEO Ashish Vaswani and describes itself as building an open platform to fuel and accelerate AI breakthroughs globally. Its research output covers pretraining data, optimizers and model releases.

The lab published Essential-Web, a 24-trillion-token organized web dataset, in June 2025 and a series of papers and posts on the Muon optimizer in May and June 2025. In December 2025 it announced Rnj-1, which it calls its first instrument of intelligence.

Mission and ambitions

Essential AI aims to build an open platform that accelerates AI progress, and it shares datasets and research as part of that effort.
