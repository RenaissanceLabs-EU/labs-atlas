About

The Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence was initiated in October 2019 and licensed by the UAE Ministry of Education in April 2020. Sir J. Michael Brady served as founding interim president, and Eric Xing has been president since January 2021. Ling Shao is founding provost and Timothy Baldwin has been provost since April 2024.

Its graduate programs launched in January 2021 and the first MSc cohort graduated in January 2023. By 2025 it had 84 faculty and more than 200 postdocs and researchers.

The Institute of Foundation Models, launched in May 2025, coordinates the Jais Arabic models and the K2 family. In September 2026 it released K2 Horizon, six open models from 0.9B to 375B parameters, with weights, code, data and methodology.

Mission and ambitions

The university aims to advance AI education and research, and its institute releases foundation models openly.
