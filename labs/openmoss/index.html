About

OpenMOSS is a research group under the Shanghai Innovation Institute, working closely with Fudan University and MOSS-AI. Its GitHub profile lists Xipeng Qiu of Fudan as an associated academic, and its site describes work on open-source large models and AGI research.

The MOSS repository was created in April 2023 and has more than 12,000 stars under Apache 2.0. More recent releases include MOSS-TTS in February 2026, MOSS-TTS-Nano in April 2026, MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize in May 2026 and MOSS-Image in October 2026.

The team also publishes world-action-model work under the EasyWAM name and maintains the Awesome-WAM list.

Mission and ambitions

OpenMOSS conducts open research on large models and general artificial intelligence and releases its code and models openly.
