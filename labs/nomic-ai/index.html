About

Nomic was founded in 2022 in New York. Its early work focused on open research and tools for understanding the data behind AI models, including the Atlas data explorer, GPT4All and the Nomic Embed models, which were released with open weights, open data and training code.

Working with engineering firms led the company to focus on drawings, specifications, codes and project data. Its platform now offers agents and an API for architecture, engineering and construction, along with a benchmark called AEC-Bench.

Coatue led a 17 million dollar Series A in 2023, and Aurecon and Arcadis joined as strategic investors in 2026.

Mission and ambitions

Nomic says AI should give experts more time to be experts, taking on repetitive work while accountable professionals stay in control of results.
