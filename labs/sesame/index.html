About

Sesame builds conversational voice AI and is co-led by Brendan Iribe, a co-founder of Oculus, and Ankit Kumar. It announced its voice companions in February 2025 and has since pursued voice-driven wearables, raising 250 million dollars in a round led by Sequoia Capital in October 2025.

In March 2025 it open-sourced CSM-1B, a base speech generation model pairing a Llama backbone with an audio decoder that produces Mimi audio codes. The release is under Apache 2.0 with ethical use guidelines.

The open checkpoint is a base model that has not been fine-tuned to a specific voice; a fine-tuned version powers the company's demo. The technical post lists Johan Schalkwyk, Ankit Kumar, Dan Lyth, Sefik Emre Eskimez, Zack Hodari, Cinjon Resnick, Ramon Sanabria and Raven Jiang.

Mission and ambitions

Sesame aims for natural, voice-based conversational AI and shares a base model with the community.
