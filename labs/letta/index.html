About

Letta grew out of MemGPT, a UC Berkeley Sky Computing Lab project on virtual context management for language model agents. Founders Charles Packer, chief executive, and Sarah Wooders, chief technology officer, met during their PhD research there under Ion Stoica and Joseph Gonzalez, who advise the company.

In September 2024 the company emerged from stealth with a 10 million dollar seed round led by Felicis, with Sunflower Capital and Essence VC participating. It released an Agent Development Environment and API platform alongside its open-source framework.

Letta describes itself as a research lab building machines that learn, aimed at agents that remember everything and improve continuously.

Mission and ambitions

Letta states that it is building machines that learn: experiential agents that remember, learn continuously and improve themselves over time.
