About

Prior Labs was founded by Frank Hutter, Noah Hollmann and Sauraj Gambhir to commercialize TabPFN, a transformer pretrained on synthetic data that handles tabular prediction in a single forward pass.

TabPFN was published in Nature in early 2025. The company raised a 9 million euro pre-seed round led by Balderton in February 2025, and its scientific advisory board includes Yann LeCun and Bernhard Schölkopf.

In May 2026 SAP announced plans to acquire Prior Labs, with commitments that it remains a separate legal entity and keeps its open-source work.

Mission and ambitions

Prior Labs wants foundation models to become the default tool for tabular data, which dominates business and science data.
