About

Kotoba Technologies was founded in 2023 by Noriyuki Kojima, chief executive, and Jungo Kasai, chief technology officer. The company is incorporated in San Francisco with a Tokyo operations office and describes itself as a frontier voice AI company with expertise in East Asian languages.

It is known for Kotoba-Whisper, distilled Whisper models for Japanese speech recognition developed with Asahi Ushio. Version 1.0 appeared in April 2024, trained on the ReazonSpeech corpus, with version 2.0 following in September 2024. Its newer Koto model targets low-latency text-to-speech, speech-to-text and speech translation that can run on-premise or on-device.

A Seed round of about 11.8 million dollars led by Globis Capital Partners was reported in 2025.

Mission and ambitions

The company aims to build real-time speech models and simultaneous translation across languages, with a focus on East Asian languages.
