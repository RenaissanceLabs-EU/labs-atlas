About

Liquid AI was spun out of MIT's Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory by Ramin Hasani, chief executive, Mathias Lechner, chief technology officer, Alexander Amini, chief scientist, and Daniela Rus. Reports date its start to 2023.

It builds Liquid Foundation Models, designed for efficient inference on phones, laptops and other edge devices. It released LFM2 in July 2025 as small open-weight models, followed by a technical report in December 2025 and the LFM2.5 and LFM2-24B-A2B models.

Weights are published on Hugging Face, and the company also sells on-premise and edge deployments.

Mission and ambitions

Liquid AI aims to build efficient general-purpose models that run on-device and on-premise, and to release open-weight versions for developers.
