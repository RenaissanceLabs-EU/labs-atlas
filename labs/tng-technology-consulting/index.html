About

TNG Technology Consulting is a Bavarian consultancy founded in 2001 with several hundred employees. Its AI research team publishes model work on Hugging Face under tngtech.

DeepSeek-R1T-Chimera, released on 27 April 2025, is constructed from parts of DeepSeek-R1 and DeepSeek-V3 mixture-of-experts models rather than fine-tuning. DeepSeek-TNG R1T2 Chimera followed on 3 July 2025 under the MIT license.

Mission and ambitions

TNG presents its model work as research on assembling new models from existing open parents.
