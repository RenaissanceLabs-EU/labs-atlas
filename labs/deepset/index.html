About

deepset began building custom language AI systems for industry in 2018. In 2020 it developed Haystack, an open-source Python framework that has become a widely used base for AI agents and retrieval applications.

The company is co-led by Milos Rusic as CEO and Malte Pietsch as CTO, both co-founders. It sells enterprise products and services built on the open-source foundation and has also released German language models and datasets.

Mission and ambitions

deepset aims to make custom AI solutions accessible to every organization by simplifying the development of LLM agents and applications.
