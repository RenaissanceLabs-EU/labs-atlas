About

fast.ai was founded in October 2016 by Jeremy Howard and Rachel Thomas with the goal of democratizing deep learning. Sylvain Gugger is also listed as a key person.

Its free course Practical Deep Learning for Coders was first taught at the University of San Francisco and is delivered online with Python as the only prerequisite. The fastai library, built on PyTorch and licensed under Apache 2.0, reached version 1.0 in the fall of 2018. In 2018 fast.ai students won Stanford's DAWNBench CIFAR-10 challenge for fastest and cheapest training.

Mission and ambitions

fast.ai works to make deep learning accessible to people from varied backgrounds, through free courses and open-source software.
