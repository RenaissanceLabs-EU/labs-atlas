About

Voxel51 was co-founded by University of Michigan professor Jason Corso and Brian Moore and is based in Ann Arbor. Sources differ on whether it began in 2016 or 2018.

FiftyOne open source launched in August 2020 and reached version 1.0 in October 2024. The commercial platform builds on it.

Mission and ambitions

Voxel51 aims to help teams build better visual AI through data-centric tooling.
