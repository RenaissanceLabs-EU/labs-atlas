About

EdinburghNLP brings together faculty, postdocs and PhD students in the School of Informatics. Its research covers language modelling, morphology, parsing, semantics, discourse, generation and machine translation, with links to speech, vision and robotics.

The group reports a faculty of more than a dozen core members and five European Research Council grants, and it is ranked first for NLP output by csrankings.org. It publishes code and data openly, including the Nematus neural machine translation toolkit, the XSum summarization dataset and a widely used list of hallucination-detection papers.

Mission and ambitions

To develop algorithms that let computers understand and produce human language, and to share the resulting tools with the research community.

Milestones

  1. 2016

    Nematus repository created

    The open-source neural machine translation system was first published on GitHub.

  2. 2017

    Nematus toolkit paper

    Sennrich, Firat, Cho, Birch, Haddow and colleagues described the toolkit on arXiv.

  3. 2018

    XSum dataset

    Narayan, Cohen and Lapata introduced topic-aware convolutional networks and the extreme summarization dataset.

  4. 2023

    Awesome hallucination detection list

    The group opened a curated list of papers on hallucination detection that became its most starred repository.

  5. 2025

    MMLongBench

    A benchmark for long-context vision-language models was released by the group.
