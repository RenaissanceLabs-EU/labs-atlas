About

Lelapa AI was founded in Johannesburg in 2022 by Pelonomi Moiloa, Jade Abbott and academic collaborators, and the name means home in Sotho and Tswana. Moiloa is chief executive and Abbott is chief technology officer, and the team page also lists Vukosi Marivate as chief information officer, Benjamin Rosman as chief scientist and George Konidaris as an advisor. Brown University and Wired have described Moiloa, Abbott, Marivate, Konidaris, Rosman and Pravesh Ranchod as the founding group.

The company's commercial product line is Vulavula, with Vulavula Transcribe for multilingual contact centres and Vulavula Translate for translation between African languages. It targets telecoms and financial services.

Lelapa also releases research models and datasets openly, notably InkubaLM, and states that it builds resource-efficient language AI designed to scale.

Mission and ambitions

Lelapa states that it builds resource-efficient language AI for global scale, starting with African languages that are poorly served by existing models.
