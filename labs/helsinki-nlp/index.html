About

Helsinki-NLP is the language technology research group at the University of Helsinki. Its research areas include cross-lingual NLP, data-intensive and neural NLP, NLP for morphologically rich languages and language technology in the humanities.

The group maintains OPUS, a collection of parallel corpora, and publishes translation models and code through its GitHub and Hugging Face organisations. It takes part in national and international research projects.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to provide open translation models and parallel data that cover many languages, including low-resource ones.
