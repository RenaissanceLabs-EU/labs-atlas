About

Open Climate Fix was founded in 2019 by Dan Travers and Jack Kelly, and its GitHub organization describes its method as open science to mitigate climate change. The team combines expertise from energy, deep learning, design and product management.

Its work includes a national solar forecast for Great Britain used daily by the National Energy System Operator, cloudcasting from satellite imagery, wind forecasting, mapping of rooftop solar and an open energy data advocacy program.

The organization reports reductions in forecasting error and cost savings for grid operators and publishes models and code on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

Open Climate Fix works toward a world powered by clean and affordable energy by delivering AI that makes renewable generation more predictable.
