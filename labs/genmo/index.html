About

Genmo is a US video generation company whose first public open-source release was Mochi 1, licensed under Apache 2.0 for individual and commercial use. Its team describes itself as including original creators of foundational AI research.

The company lists investors and advisors including Ion Stoica, Pieter Abbeel, Joseph Gonzalez, Amjad Masad and Michele Catasta. Weights and code are published on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Mission and ambitions

Genmo aims to advance video generation and to share an open model with the research and developer community.
