About

Epoch AI began in 2021 as a group of volunteers and became a formal organization after its 2022 paper on training compute trends. It describes its aim as helping people understand AI from a neutral perspective grounded in the best available evidence.

Its public databases cover ML hardware, training compute estimates for AI models, AI investment and companies, GPU clusters and AI data centers. It also publishes analysis, chip sales data and polling on AI usage.

Mission and ambitions

Epoch AI seeks to improve society's understanding of the drivers, progress and impact of AI, using data to inform decisions about the technology.
