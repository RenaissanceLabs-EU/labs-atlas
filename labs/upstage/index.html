About

Upstage was founded in October 2020 by Sung Kim, a former professor at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology who previously led Naver's Clova team. It builds language models and document AI for enterprises.

Its compact Solar 10.7B model was released in December 2023, followed by Solar Pro 2 in July 2025. Some models are published openly, though later releases are under the Upstage Solar License.

Mission and ambitions

Upstage aims to build efficient models and document AI for enterprises.
