About

Answer.AI announced itself in December 2023 in a post by Jeremy Howard describing a new kind of R&D lab that builds practical products from foundational research. In November 2024 fast.ai announced that it was joining the lab.

Its output is mostly open source. Projects include a method for training 70B models on consumer GPUs with FSDP and QLoRA, the FastHTML web framework, the llms.txt proposal, and ModernBERT, an encoder model released in December 2024.

The lab's team includes Jeremy Howard, Hamel Husain, Benjamin Clavié, Johno Whitaker and Benjamin Warner among others.

Mission and ambitions

Answer.AI describes its aim as creating practical end-user products based on foundational research breakthroughs.
