About

TAIDE, the Trustworthy AI Dialogue Engine, launched on 27 April 2023. The National Science and Technology Council acts as guiding agency, and the National Institutes of Applied Research implements the work.

The project has released the TAIDE-LX-7B model in 2024, Llama 3 and Llama 3.1 based 8B chat models, and Gemma 3 based 12B chat models in 2025 and 2026. A small embedding model, Embeddinggemma-GTAIDE-300m, appeared in 2026.

Its models are intended to serve Traditional Chinese users and Taiwanese institutions.

Mission and ambitions

TAIDE aims to provide a trustworthy Traditional Chinese language model base for Taiwan's public and private sectors.

  3. 2025

    Llama3.1-TAIDE-LX-8B-Chat

    8B chat model with a 131K context length.

  4. 2025

    Gemma-3-TAIDE-12b-Chat

    12B chat model based on Gemma 3.

  5. 2026

    Embeddinggemma-GTAIDE-300m

    Embedding model aimed at retrieval of Taiwanese legal regulations.
