About

Swallow is led by researchers in the Okazaki and Yokota laboratories at the Institute of Science Tokyo, together with the National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST). Naoaki Okazaki leads the project and the pre-training corpus team, Rio Yokota leads LLM training, and Sakae Mizuki leads instruction tuning and evaluation.

The project adapts existing open models such as Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Qwen and gpt-oss to Japanese through continual pretraining and instruction tuning. Its model list runs from the first Swallow models to Llama 3.3 Swallow 70B, Qwen3 Swallow and GPT-OSS Swallow.

Weights are published on Hugging Face under the tokyotech-llm organization, and the project documents its corpus and evaluation work for the Japanese community.

Mission and ambitions

Swallow aims to build and openly release Japanese large language models and the corpora and evaluation methods needed to improve them.
