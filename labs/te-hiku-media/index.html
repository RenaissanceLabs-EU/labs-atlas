About

Te Hiku Media, formally Te Reo Irirangi o Te Hiku o Te Ika, is an iwi radio and media organization in Kaitaia whose site dates its roots to 1991. It runs Te Hiku Tech, which builds tools for te reo Māori.

Its Kōrero Māori campaign collected labelled speech under the Kaitiakitanga License, which restricts use of the data to the benefit of Māori people. The organization reported a speech recognizer for te reo Māori built with DeepSpeech, with accuracy of about 92 percent.

All content on its site is covered by the same licence, and contributors retain ownership of their data.

Mission and ambitions

Te Hiku Media aims to revitalize te reo Māori with technology that Māori communities own and control.
