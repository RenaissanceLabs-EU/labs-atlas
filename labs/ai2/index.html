About

The Allen Institute for AI, known as Ai2, was founded in 2014 by Microsoft co-founder Paul Allen. Oren Etzioni led it for nine years until 2022, and Ali Farhadi became CEO in July 2023.

Its early work included the Semantic Scholar search engine, released in November 2015, and the Aristo project, which passed an eighth-grade science exam in 2018. Since 2023 it has concentrated on open language models.

The OLMo models and the Dolma corpus were released in February 2024 with data, code and checkpoints, and the Tulu post-training recipes reached version 3 in November 2024.

Mission and ambitions

Ai2 pursues AI research and engineering for the common good, with open release of the models, data and code behind its work.
