About

Stability AI was founded by Emad Mostaque, who served as CEO until March 2024. Prem Akkaraju became CEO in June 2024, with Sean Parker as executive chairman, after a restructuring.

Stable Diffusion 1.5 (October 2022) became a foundation for community fine-tuning. Stable Diffusion 3.5 followed in late 2024 under a community licence free for those with under $1M annual revenue.

Not all releases are permissively licensed.

Mission and ambitions

Stability aims to provide generative models across modalities with open weights.
