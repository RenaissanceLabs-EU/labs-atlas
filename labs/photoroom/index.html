About

Photoroom was co-founded in 2019 by Matt, a former GoPro product manager, and engineer Eliot Andres, after frustration with slow tools for cutting out objects from photos. It builds an image-editing app and an API used for e-commerce visuals.

In 2025 it began publishing its own text-to-image models, called PRX, together with an account of the architecture, training and post-training process. The 1.3 billion parameter model was trained from scratch and is available through the Diffusers library.

A later pixel-space variant, PRX Pixel, uses raw images without a VAE and also carries an Apache 2.0 licence.

Mission and ambitions

Photoroom wants to show the full process of training production-grade image models and to share it so others can build on it.
