About

Reka was founded in 2022 by researchers formerly at DeepMind and elsewhere in industry, and builds multimodal models. Dani Yogatama, also an associate professor at USC, is cofounder and CEO. Its flagship models are served through its own platform, while selected models are released openly.

In March 2025 it released Reka Flash 3, a 21B reasoning model trained with reinforcement learning, under Apache 2.0. In July 2025 it open-sourced Reka Flash 3.1 together with the Reka Quant quantization method.

It also publishes the Vibe-Eval benchmark for multimodal models, and a 2024 technical report describes its Core, Flash and Edge models.

Mission and ambitions

Reka aims to build capable multimodal models and to share part of its work with the community.
