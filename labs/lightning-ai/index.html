About

Lightning AI was founded by William Falcon, who began developing PyTorch Lightning in 2015 at Columbia University and open-sourced it in 2019 while completing a PhD at NYU and working at Facebook AI Research. His advisors were Kyunghyun Cho and Yann LeCun.

The company's open-source tools include PyTorch Lightning, Fabric, LitGPT, LitServe, TorchMetrics and the Thunder compiler. In 2023 it launched Lightning AI Studio, a browser-based cloud platform for coding, training and deploying models.

In 2026 Lightning AI and the GPU cloud provider Voltage Park combined into one company, offering a platform that spans GPUs to developer tools.

Mission and ambitions

Lightning AI aims to give developers one platform from hardware to tooling for building AI, while maintaining its widely used open-source frameworks.
