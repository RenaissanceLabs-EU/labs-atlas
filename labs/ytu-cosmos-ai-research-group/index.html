About

COSMOS is in the Computer Engineering Department of Yildiz Technical University.

It released cosmosGPT in 2024 and Turkish-Llama and Turkish-Gemma models.

Models are on Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

The group builds Turkish-first language models.
