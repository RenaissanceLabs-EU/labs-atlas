About

The Alan Turing Institute was founded in 2015 as the UK's national institute for data science, and AI was added to its remit in 2017. It is named after Alan Turing and is largely government funded.

Its researchers and engineers publish many open repositories on GitHub. Since 2025 the institute has focused on defence and national security at the government's request.

In May 2026 George Williamson, previously of HMGCC, was named chief executive.

Mission and ambitions

The institute's stated direction is now to serve UK sovereign capability in AI, defence and security.
