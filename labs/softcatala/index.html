About

Softcatala was created in 1998 by volunteers to promote Catalan in computing and on the Internet. Jordi Mas is named as its founder. Early work focused on translating proprietary software before shifting toward free software.

It operates a machine translation service, available since 2000, which moved from a non-free engine to Apertium. The group helps maintain Apertium language pairs by adding vocabulary and rules.

It publishes models and datasets on Hugging Face and code on GitHub, and has a long record of localised versions of popular programs.

Mission and ambitions

The association aims to ensure Catalan speakers can use technology fully in their own language.
