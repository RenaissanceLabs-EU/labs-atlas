About

The Alexandra Institute was established in Aarhus in 1999 by business people, researchers and public representatives to link Danish companies with university IT research. It became an approved technological service provider in 2007 and has been owned by the Aarhus University Research Foundation since 2012.

Its open source work is published on GitHub and Hugging Face. DaNLP collected Danish datasets and models for tagging, named entity recognition, sentiment and hate speech detection, with a NoDaLiDa 2021 paper; the repository was archived in March 2025. The ScandEval benchmark, described in arXiv 2304.00906, later grew into EuroEval.

The institute serves Danish companies and public bodies and works on language technology, evaluation and responsible AI.

Mission and ambitions

The institute applies IT research to the needs of Danish business and society and publishes language resources so that Danish remains well supported in AI.

Milestones

  1. 1999

    Institute founded

    The Alexandra Institute was established in Aarhus to connect business and IT research.

  2. 2007

    GTS approval

    It was approved as a technological service provider in Denmark.

  3. 2019

    DaNLP repository

    The Danish NLP resource repository was created on GitHub.

  4. 2021

    DaNLP paper

    The DaNLP paper by Pauli, Barrett, Lacroix and Hvingelby was presented at NoDaLiDa.

  5. 2023

    ScandEval

    The Scandinavian language model benchmark was described in arXiv 2304.00906.

  6. 2025

    EuroEval

    The benchmark continued under the EuroEval name, with a repository created in September 2025.
