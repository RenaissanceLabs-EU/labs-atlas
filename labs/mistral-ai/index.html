About

Mistral AI was founded in April 2023 in Paris by Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample and Timothée Lacroix, who had met at École polytechnique and worked at Google DeepMind and Meta. Its first model, Mistral 7B, was released on 27 September 2023 under the Apache 2.0 license.

The company builds language, coding, speech, document and forecasting models, and sells them through its Studio and Vibe products and through sovereign deployments for enterprises and governments. Recent open-weight releases include Mistral Small 4 under Apache 2.0, Voxtral TTS and the one-trillion-parameter Mistral Large 4.

Mistral is venture-funded and commercial. ASML took an 11 percent shareholding in 2025, and the firm is often cited as a leading beneficiary of European digital sovereignty policy.

Mission and ambitions

Mistral says its goal is to put frontier AI in everyone's hands, keeping it open and giving organizations control and ownership of their AI systems.

Milestones

  1. 2023

    Founding in Paris

    Mensch, Lample and Lacroix established the company in April 2023.

  2. 2023

    Mistral 7B

    A 7.3-billion-parameter model released under Apache 2.0 that outperformed Llama 2 13B on the benchmarks Mistral reported.

  3. 2025

    Magistral Small

    The first open reasoning model from Mistral, released in June 2025.

  4. 2025

    Mistral Large 3 and Ministral 3

    A 675-billion-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model and three small dense models released in December 2025.

  5. 2026

    Mistral Small 4

    A multimodal hybrid model with a 256,000-token context window released under Apache 2.0 in March 2026.

  6. 2026

    Mistral Large 4

    A one-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model whose weights were released on 29 October 2026.
