About

Probabl was formed in 2023 and launched publicly in early 2024 to give the scikit-learn ecosystem a sustainable institutional home. Scikit-learn core maintainers who had been employed by Inria joined as co-founders, and Yann Lechelle, formerly of Scaleway, became chief executive.

Inria and the French state are shareholders, and the company has a mission-driven legal structure. Its stated aim is the sustainable development of a data science commons, with professional services and products built around open-source libraries such as scikit-learn and skrub.

In October 2025 Sifted reported a 13 million euro seed round. In January 2026 Gaël Varoquaux, a scikit-learn co-founder and Inria researcher, announced he was taking the role of chief science officer.

Mission and ambitions

Probabl intends to make open-source machine learning tools sustainable and to offer professional services and products around them.
