About

TakeLab is a research group at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing, University of Zagreb, focusing on AI, machine learning and NLP.

Professor Jan Snajder is a prominent member. Work includes Croatian morphological analysis and SemEval shared task entries.

Code is published on GitHub.

Mission and ambitions

The lab advances AI and NLP research, with attention to Croatian.
