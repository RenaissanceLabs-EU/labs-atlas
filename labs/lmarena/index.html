About

Chatbot Arena was released on 24 April 2023 by researchers around UC Berkeley. Its founders are Wei-Lin Chiang, Anastasios N. Angelopoulos and Ion Stoica. The site moved to its own domain, lmarena.ai, in September 2024.

LMArena incorporated as an independent company in April 2025 and raised a 100 million dollar seed round that May, a 150 million dollar Series A in January 2026 and a 200 million dollar Series B in October 2026. On 28 January 2026 it rebranded as Arena. The project originated in the LMSYS group, which also produced FastChat and RouteLLM.

The platform ranks models through human pairwise votes and publishes evaluation data and tools on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to provide transparent, community-driven evaluation of AI models using real user preferences.
