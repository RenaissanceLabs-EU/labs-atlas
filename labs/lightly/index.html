About

Lightly was founded by Matthias Heller and Igor Susmelj as a data-centric AI company based on ETH Zurich research. It raised a 3 million dollar seed round in May 2022 and reports 10 million dollars raised in total.

Its open-source lightly library for self-supervised learning dates from October 2020. It later added the labelformat converter, LightlyTrain for vision model pretraining and distillation, and LightlyStudio, alongside a commercial platform.

Mission and ambitions

To help teams build better vision models by improving the data that models are trained on.
