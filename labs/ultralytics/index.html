About

Ultralytics is a computer-vision company led by founder and CEO Glenn Jocher. Its ultralytics Python package provides YOLO models for detection, segmentation and other tasks.

Models are distributed under AGPL-3.0 by default, with a paid Enterprise license for private deployment. YOLO11 was released in September 2024.

Mission and ambitions

Ultralytics aims to make vision AI accessible through a widely used open library.
