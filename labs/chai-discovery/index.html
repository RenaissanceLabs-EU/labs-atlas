About

Chai Discovery is a drug-discovery company founded in 2024 by Joshua Meier (chief executive), Jack Dent (president), Matthew McPartlon (chief technology officer) and Jacques Boitreaud. Its team came from OpenAI, Meta FAIR, Stripe and Google X, and it describes its work as predicting and reprogramming interactions between biomolecules.

Chai-1, released on 9 September 2024, predicts structures of proteins, small molecules, DNA and RNA. The company published weights and inference code under Apache 2.0 and reported 77 percent success on PoseBusters against 76 percent for AlphaFold3, with competitive results even when run without multiple sequence alignments.

Chai-2, announced in 2025 for de novo antibody design, is a separate model that sources describe as not openly released. Funding has included a $30 million seed round, a $70 million Series A in August 2025 led by Menlo Ventures and a $130 million Series B reported in January 2026.

Mission and ambitions

Chai aims to let scientists design medicines computationally by predicting and engineering molecular interactions.
