About

The Language Technology Group (LTG) is part of the Department of Informatics at the University of Oslo, led by Lilja Øvrelid. Its members include Stephan Oepen, Erik Velldal, Andrey Kutuzov, Yves Scherrer, Pierre Lison and David Samuel. The group works on natural language processing and large language models, including for Norwegian.

It released NorBERT in January 2021 and NorBERT 2 in February 2022, then the NorBERT 3 family and the NorBench benchmark in 2023. The group has also trained generative Norwegian models such as NorMistral and NorBLOOM. Projects it participates in include HPLT, OpenEuroLLM, NorwAI, Integreat and MediaFutures.

The group coordinates the Nordic Language Processing Laboratory and publishes code and models on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

LTG develops language technology and language models, with a focus on Norwegian and openly available resources for evaluation and training.

Milestones

  1. 2021

    NorBERT

    Released the first large-scale Norwegian contextualised language model, described at NoDaLiDa 2021.

  2. 2022

    NorBERT 2

    Released an updated model trained on about 15 billion words of C4 and NCC.

  3. 2023

    NorBERT 3 and NorBench

    Released a family of four model sizes together with a benchmark for Norwegian.

  4. 2023

    NorMistral and NorBLOOM

    Trained generative Norwegian language models.
