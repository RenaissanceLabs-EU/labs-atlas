About

Preferred Networks was established in March 2014 as a spin-off from Preferred Infrastructure, founded in 2006. Toru Nishikawa is CEO and Daisuke Okanohara is COO.

In June 2015 the company released Chainer, an open-source deep learning framework that influenced dynamic-graph design; development ended in December 2019 as the team moved to PyTorch. It announced the MN-Core accelerator in December 2018, and the MN-3 system topped the Green500 list in June 2020.

Preferred Networks continues to publish open code and Japanese language models, including the PLaMo family.

Mission and ambitions

The company aims to apply deep learning to real-world problems, combining software, custom hardware and large-scale computing.
