About

Motif Technologies is a Korean AI company reported to have been established in February 2025 as a spin-off from the AI infrastructure company Moreh. It trains its own foundation models, led by CEO Junghwan Lim according to press reports and its technical report.

Its Hugging Face organization carries the Motif model family, including the Motif-2-12.7B open-weight model described in a November 2025 technical report, as well as audio, vision encoder, video and VAE components. The report describes a new attention design, a curriculum-based data schedule and optimized training kernels.

Motif-3, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with about 314 billion parameters, was published in August 2026 under a non-commercial research license, a departure from the Apache 2.0 license used for Motif-2. Press reports link its training to South Korea's national sovereign-AI program.

Mission and ambitions

Motif aims to build competitive, domestically developed foundation models and the efficient training methods behind them.
