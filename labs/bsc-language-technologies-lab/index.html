About

The Language Technologies unit at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, led by Marta Villegas, develops resources and models for Spanish, Catalan and other European languages.

ALIA, a public, open and multilingual AI infrastructure coordinated by BSC, was announced in January 2025. Its Salamandra models, in 2B, 7B and 40B sizes, are published on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 and were trained on MareNostrum 5.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to build open language technology for Spain's languages with public funding.
