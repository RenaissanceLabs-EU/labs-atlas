About

Unsloth was started by brothers Daniel and Michael Han, who launched it on 30 November 2023 as an open-source tool for faster single-GPU fine-tuning. They began in Sydney and later moved to San Francisco.

It joined Y Combinator's Summer 2024 batch and also publishes quantized model uploads.

Mission and ambitions

Unsloth aims to make training and running LLMs cheaper for individual developers.
