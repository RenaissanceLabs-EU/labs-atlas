About

Mila, the Quebec AI Institute, traces its origin to the lab Yoshua Bengio founded at the Université de Montréal in 1993. In 2017 it grew into a collaboration between the Université de Montréal and McGill University with Polytechnique Montréal and HEC Montréal, and it became a non-profit in 2018.

The institute brings together academic researchers and students working on deep learning, reinforcement learning, health, climate, responsible AI and related areas. It reports a community of more than 1,400 people and runs training programs, an AI policy fellowship and a venture program alongside research.

Mila lists 34 open-source projects, including Theano, an early deep learning framework that originated there, SpeechBrain, TorchDrug, BabyAI and Orion.

Mission and ambitions

Mila aims to be a global hub for scientific advances that drive AI innovation for everyone's benefit, built on collaboration, excellence and ethics.

Milestones

  1. 1993

    Founding of the lab

    Yoshua Bengio founded the Montreal lab that later became Mila.

  2. 2017

    Mila as a university partnership

    The institute expanded through a collaboration of Université de Montréal and McGill University with Polytechnique Montréal and HEC Montréal.

  3. 2017

    End of Theano development

    Development of Theano, an early deep learning framework from Mila and the Université de Montréal, was completed.

  4. 2018

    Non-profit status

    Mila became a non-profit organization.

  5. 2019

    Move to new premises

    Mila moved into its current building in Montreal's Mile-Ex neighbourhood.
