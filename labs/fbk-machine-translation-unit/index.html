About

The MT group at Fondazione Bruno Kessler works on speech and text technologies for multilingual communication. Its research covers neural architectures and multimodal resources for applications such as subtitling, interpreting and gender inclusivity, and it sits within FBK's Digital Industry center.

The group's site lists corpora including MCIF, mGeNTE, MOSEL, Speech-MASSIVE and WAGS, and software such as simulstream. Researchers named on the site include Luisa Bentivogli and Beatrice Savoldi.

Mission and ambitions

The unit aims to advance speech and text translation, with attention to inclusivity, and to share its data and code openly.
