About

The Munich Digitization Center of the Bavarian State Library runs the dbmdz GitHub organization, which began in 2016 and mixes digital library software with open language models. Its berts repository, started in September 2019, documents BERT, DistilBERT, ELECTRA, GPT-2 and ConvBERT models.

Models trained on Europeana newspapers cover German, French and historic multilingual text, with German BERT released in February 2020. All models are MIT-licensed on Hugging Face under the dbmdz organization.

Mission and ambitions

To make digital library collections and language models for historical and modern text openly usable.

  2. 2020

  3. 2020

    German GPT-2 and Europeana French

    A German GPT-2 and French BERT and ELECTRA models were released.

  4. 2021

    Historic language models

    Multilingual and monolingual historic models were published.

  5. 2021

    Historic Dutch BERT

    A model trained on Delpher newspapers from 1618 to 1879 was released.
