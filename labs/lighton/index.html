About

LightOn was founded in Paris in 2016 by Igor Carron, Laurent Daudet, Florent Krzakala and Sylvain Gigan. Carron is chief executive and Daudet is deputy managing director. It launched an optical processing unit in 2018, and an OPU was integrated into the Jean Zay supercomputer in 2021.

The company began training language models in 2020 and says it has trained more than twelve since. It has open-sourced PAGnol for French, RITA for proteins, Mambaoutai, and models built with AnswerAI such as ModernBERT, plus retrieval models like GTE-ModernColBERT and tools such as PyLate and FastPlaid.

In November 2024 LightOn listed on Euronext Growth Paris and describes itself as Europe's first listed generative AI startup. Its commercial product is Paradigm, an on-premise enterprise platform.

Mission and ambitions

LightOn builds enterprise search and document understanding on private infrastructure while publishing retrieval models and tools as open source.
