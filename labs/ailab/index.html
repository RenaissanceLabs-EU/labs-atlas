About

The Artificial Intelligence Laboratory belongs to the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Latvia in Riga and has conducted research since 1992. Its work covers natural language processing and machine learning, including Latvian speech recognition and synthesis, syntactic and semantic analysis and language models.

The lab maintains a set of public Latvian resources, among them Tezaurs.lv, Korpuss.lv, the LV-PIPE tools, CLARIN-LV and Balsutalka.lv. Its FullStack-LV repository offers a multilayer corpus with Universal Dependencies, FrameNet, PropBank and AMR layers, released under a Creative Commons licence.

Funding comes from the European Union, the European Regional Development Fund and Latvian ministries and councils. The lab has partnered with the news agency LETA, Deutsche Welle and the National Library of Latvia.

Mission and ambitions

The lab states that it develops resources and technologies that help Latvian thrive in a multilingual environment. It publishes its tools and data openly for researchers and developers.

Milestones

  1. 2015

    Tezaurs repository

    The open Latvian lexical database repository was created on GitHub in April 2015.

  2. 2016

    SemEval 2016 semantic analysis

    The lab reports the best result in the semantic analysis task at SemEval 2016.

  3. 2017

    SemEval 2017 text generation

    The lab reports the best result in the text generation task at SemEval 2017.

  4. 2018

    FullStack-LV

    A multilayer Latvian corpus with UD, FrameNet, PropBank and AMR layers was started and published at LREC 2018.

  5. 2020

    LVBERT

    A pretrained Latvian BERT model repository was created in July 2020.

  6. 2023

    MultiCoNER 2023

    The lab placed third among European teams in the named entity recognition task.
