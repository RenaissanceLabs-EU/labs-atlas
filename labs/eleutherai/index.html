About

EleutherAI began in July 2020 as a Discord server under the working name LibreAI. Connor Leahy, Leo Gao and Sid Black are named as founders, and the group was renamed EleutherAI later that month. In early 2023 it was incorporated as a non-profit research institute.

It first used compute from Google's TPU Research Cloud and later from CoreWeave. The group released The Pile in December 2020, followed by the GPT-Neo, GPT-J and GPT-NeoX-20B models, and the Pythia suite for studying training dynamics.

Today it reports about fifteen full- and part-time staff along with regular volunteers. Its lm-evaluation-harness, the most starred repository in its GitHub organization, is used widely to benchmark language models.

Mission and ambitions

To advance interpretability and alignment research on open foundation models, keep such models open to study beyond a few companies, and educate the public about their capabilities and risks.

Milestones

  1. 2020

    The Pile released

    An 800GB dataset of diverse text for language modeling, authored by Gao, Biderman, Black and others.

  2. 2021

    GPT-Neo

    The first GPT-Neo models of 125M, 1.3B and 2.7B parameters were published in March 2021.

  3. 2021

    GPT-J-6B

    A six-billion-parameter open model was released in June 2021.

  4. 2022

    GPT-NeoX-20B

    A 20-billion-parameter model trained with CoreWeave compute was released in February 2022.

  5. 2023

    Pythia suite and incorporation

    Pythia provided checkpoints for analyzing models across training and scaling, and the group incorporated as a non-profit institute.

  6. 2025

    Common Pile v0.1

    A training dataset built to exclude copyrighted material was released in June 2025.

Open source

GitHubgithub.com/EleutherAI
  • lm-evaluation-harness

    Most-starred repo; the standard few-shot LLM evaluation framework.

  • gpt-neo

    Model-parallel GPT-2/GPT-3-style training library from the early GPT-Neo era.

  • gpt-neox

    Large-scale autoregressive training library behind GPT-NeoX-20B and Pythia.

  • pythia

    Hub for the Pythia suite and learning-dynamics research.
