About

Maincode is an AI lab founded in Melbourne in late 2024. It describes itself as an independent Australian lab that runs its own hardware, including MC-2, an AMD MI355X cluster in Melbourne, with an inference engine written in Rust and a further cluster, MC-3, planned.

Its product is Matilda, offered for chat, coding, desktop and API use with paid plans from 25 dollars a month. Maincode has also released Maincoder-1B, which it says scored 76 percent on HumanEval, the highest among open 1B models in its December 2025 comparison.

The company's founders are not named on its homepage.

Mission and ambitions

Maincode states that it builds AI in Australia on its own infrastructure and releases some models openly.
