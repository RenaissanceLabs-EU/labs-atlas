About

Goodfire says it was founded by researchers who helped pioneer interpretability at OpenAI and Google DeepMind. Its team includes founding members of those efforts, professors on leave and engineers, and it reports raising over 200 million dollars from investors including Menlo, Lightspeed and B Capital.

Its stated mission is to advance the research and technology needed to understand and align AI so that people can trust superintelligence. Its Hugging Face and GitHub organisations host sparse autoencoders and other interpretability artifacts.

Mission and ambitions

Goodfire aims to make the internal workings of AI models understandable so they can be aligned and trusted.
