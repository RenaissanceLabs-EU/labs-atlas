About

The Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence was founded in November 2018, with Zhang Hongjiang listed as its founder.

It announced the Wudao 2.0 pretrained model system, and it maintains the FlagAI toolkit and the FlagOpen open-source ecosystem. It also publishes the BGE embedding models and the Emu3 multimodal models on Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

BAAI aims to advance foundational AI research in China and share models, data and tools with the wider community.
