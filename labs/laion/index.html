About

LAION, the Large-scale Artificial Intelligence Open Network, is a non-profit research network based in Germany that makes machine learning resources available to the public. It is supported by donations and public research grants. Its volunteers include Christoph Schuhmann, Jenia Jitsev and Romain Beaumont.

In 2021 it released LAION-400M, an image-text dataset recreating the kind of data used for CLIP, followed by LAION-5B in 2022, the largest openly available image-text dataset at the time. Stable Diffusion was trained on it, and LAION members helped train OpenCLIP models on it. In 2023 it co-organised the OpenAssistant crowd-sourced conversation project.

After Stanford researchers reported suspected abuse material links in LAION-5B in December 2023, LAION withdrew its datasets and in August 2024 released a cleaned Re-LAION-5B.

Mission and ambitions

LAION states that it makes large-scale machine learning resources, including datasets, tools and open models, available to the public for research and education.

Milestones

  1. 2021

    LAION-400M

    Released 400 million image-text pairs filtered with CLIP.

  2. 2022

    LAION-5B

    Released over 5 billion image-text pairs, used to train Stable Diffusion.

  3. 2022

    Reproducible scaling laws

    Published OpenCLIP scaling-law research using LAION-2B.

  4. 2023

    OpenAssistant

    Released an open chat assistant and dataset built by volunteers.

  5. 2023

    Dataset takedown

    Removed LAION-5B and LAION-400M temporarily after a report of illegal content.

  6. 2024

    Re-LAION-5B

    Released a cleaned version of the dataset.
