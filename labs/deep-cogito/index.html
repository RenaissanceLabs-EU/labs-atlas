About

Deep Cogito was founded in San Francisco in 2024 by Drishan Arora, who led language-model work for generative search at Google, and Dhruv Malhotra, a former product lead at Google DeepMind. It describes its goal as building general superintelligence and argues that this requires advances in reasoning and iterative self-improvement.

The lab introduced iterated distillation and amplification (IDA) with the Cogito v1 Preview models in April 2025, followed by Cogito v2 Preview in July 2025. Cogito v2.1, released in November 2025 as a 671 billion parameter open-weight model, was post-trained from an openly licensed DeepSeek base model.

In August 2026 the company announced a $43 million Series A led by TQ Ventures, for total funding above $56 million. Reports say the money will fund hiring, training infrastructure, future open-weight Cogito models and an enterprise business.

Mission and ambitions

Deep Cogito pursues models that improve through repeated cycles of search and distillation. It releases open weights as part of that research program.
