About

AI Sweden describes itself as a national centre for applied AI. It is not-for-profit and works with more than 180 partners from companies, public bodies and universities.

Its programmes cover AI adoption and training, sector initiatives such as healthcare and automotive, a startup programme and research on infrastructure, secure AI and language technology.

In language technology it developed GPT-SW3 with RISE and WASP, a family of autoregressive models for Swedish and the other Nordic languages, with model cards dated December 2022 and an open release on Hugging Face reported in late 2023.

Mission and ambitions

AI Sweden states that its purpose is to accelerate the use of AI for the benefit of society.
