About

Latam-GPT is a regional initiative to build a language model entirely within Latin America, for Latin America. It is coordinated by CENIA, the Chilean national AI centre, whose director Álvaro Soto spoke for the project at its launch. Reports describe a network of more than thirty institutions across the region.

Chile launched the model on 10 February 2026 as open infrastructure for developers, trained on more than eight terabytes of Latin American data in Spanish and Portuguese, with indigenous languages planned. Press reports give its cost as about 550,000 dollars, funded by CAF and CENIA, with a first version trained on cloud infrastructure and later versions planned for a supercomputer at the University of Tarapacá.

Models, data and tools are published on GitHub and Hugging Face, and the model is free for companies and public institutions to build on.

Mission and ambitions

The project aims to give the region a model that represents its languages, culture and context, and to let developers build region-specific applications without depending on models made elsewhere.
