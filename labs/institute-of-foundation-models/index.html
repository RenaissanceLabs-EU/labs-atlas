About

The Institute of Foundation Models (IFM) is a research unit of the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence in Abu Dhabi, launched publicly in May 2025. The LLM360 project, which states it is proudly sponsored by the institute, publishes open large language models together with their training data, code and checkpoints. The LLM360 Hugging Face organization notes that its namespace has moved to IFM.

LLM360's releases include Amber-7B, Crystal-7B, the 65-billion-parameter K2 trained on 1.4 trillion tokens, and TxT360, a pre-training dataset that deduplicates 99 Common Crawl snapshots and 14 other sources. In September 2026 the institute released K2 Horizon, six fully open models from 0.9 to 375 billion parameters.

The institute also coordinates the Jais Arabic models and operates with a Silicon Valley lab alongside its Abu Dhabi base.

Mission and ambitions

LLM360 aims to enable community-owned AI through open large model research, creating standards and tools for capability and knowledge transfer. The institute releases weights, code, data and training details.

Milestones

  1. 2023

    Amber-7B and Crystal-7B

    Released open 7B models with full training artifacts and chat variants.

  2. 2024

    K2-65B

    Released a 65B model trained on 1.4 trillion tokens that LLM360 reports matches Llama 2 70B with less compute.

  3. 2024

    TxT360

    Released a pre-training dataset globally deduplicating 99 Common Crawl snapshots and 14 other sources.

  4. 2025

    Institute launch

    MBZUAI launched the Institute of Foundation Models and a Silicon Valley lab.

  5. 2026

    K2 Horizon

    Released six fully open models from 0.9B to 375B parameters with data and methodology.
