About

NXAI was set up in early 2024 in Linz, Austria, by Sepp Hochreiter, who leads the Institute for Machine Learning at JKU Linz, together with the company builder Netural X and PIERER Digital Holding. Hochreiter is Chief Scientist and Albert Ortig is CEO, according to press coverage.

The company aims to turn research on sequence modeling into industrial applications in engineering, robotics, automation and process control. It publishes the xLSTM architecture code, FlashRNN kernels, Vision-LSTM and the TiRex family of zero-shot time-series forecasting models.

Its GitHub organization dates from May 2024, and recent Hugging Face releases include TiRex-2 forecasting assets and KronLSTM language models.

Mission and ambitions

NXAI states that its mission is to turn pioneering research into market-relevant industrial AI by building scalable, generalist foundation models.
