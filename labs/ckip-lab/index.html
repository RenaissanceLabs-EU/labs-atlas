About

CKIP, the Chinese Knowledge and Information Processing group, is a cross-institute research team of Academia Sinica's Institute of Information Science and Institute of Linguistics. The group dates itself to 1986 and aims to supply resources and research environments for Chinese natural language processing.

Its outputs include word segmentation, parsing, a sentence structure treebank, the Sinica Corpus, the E-HowNet lexical knowledge base, a Kangxi Dictionary ontology, language models and Chinese embeddings. Current research covers deep learning, knowledge representation, language understanding and knowledge extraction.

CKIP Transformers provides traditional Chinese ALBERT, BERT and GPT-2 models with tools for word segmentation, part-of-speech tagging and named entity recognition, distributed through GitHub, Hugging Face and PyPI under GPL-3.0. Wei-Yun Ma, an associate research fellow, is principal investigator of the lab.

Mission and ambitions

CKIP works to provide computational resources and research environments for Chinese language processing.
