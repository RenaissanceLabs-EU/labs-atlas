About

Black Forest Labs was founded in 2024 in Freiburg im Breisgau by Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann and Patrick Esser, former Stability AI researchers who had worked on latent diffusion at LMU Munich.

FLUX.1 appeared in August 2024 and FLUX.1 Kontext in May 2025, followed by FLUX.2 in November 2025. FLUX.1 Schnell is Apache 2.0, while Dev variants are open weights under non-commercial licences and Pro models are API only.

Early investors included Andreessen Horowitz, and a reported 450 million dollar financing followed in late 2025.

Mission and ambitions

The lab aims to advance generative image and video models while offering part of its work as open weights.
