About

NLX is the Natural Language and Speech Group at the Department of Informatics of the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon, led by António Branco. Together with the PORTULAN CLARIN infrastructure it publishes language resources and tools for Portuguese.

Its Hugging Face organisation PORTULAN hosts the Albertina encoder family in European and Brazilian Portuguese, from 100 million to 1.5 billion parameters, and the Gervasio decoder models at 8 and 70 billion parameters.

The group also publishes benchmark datasets such as extraglue and glue-ptpt and maintains a leaderboard of open language models for Portuguese.

Mission and ambitions

The group works to give Portuguese, in both its European and Brazilian varieties, open neural models and evaluation resources of high quality.

Milestones

  1. 2023

    Albertina PT-* introduced

    DeBERTa-based encoders for European and Brazilian Portuguese were released with an arXiv paper in May.

  2. 2023

    glue-ptpt and parlamento-pt datasets published

    Datasets released on the PORTULAN Hugging Face organisation on 12 May.

  3. 2024

    Albertina family extended

    SIGUL 2024 paper added 1.5B and 100M parameter models.

  4. 2024

    extraglue datasets released

    extraglue-instruct appeared in March and extraglue in December.
