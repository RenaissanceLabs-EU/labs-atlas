About

NorwAI is the Norwegian Research Center for AI Innovation at NTNU in Trondheim, started in October 2020 and directed by Professor Jon Atle Gulla. It combines academic research with industry partners that apply AI.

In May 2024 the centre launched a suite of Norwegian large language models, built by adapting open architectures such as Mistral and Llama to Norwegian data and trained on NTNU's Idun cluster.

Models are hosted on Hugging Face and can be downloaded free of charge for research and experiments. The centre states it has neither the mandate nor the resources to serve them as a permanent public API.

Mission and ambitions

NorwAI aims to build Norwegian-language AI capacity and to carry research results into Norwegian industry and public services.

Milestones

  1. 2020

    NorwAI starts operations

    The centre began work in October 2020.

  2. 2023

    Large Norwegian GPT model announced

    In January 2023 NorwAI described plans for a 20-billion-parameter Norwegian model trained on NTNU's Idun cluster, with partners including Schibsted and DnB.

  3. 2024

    Norwegian LLM suite launched

    Models adapted from open architectures such as Mistral and Llama were published on Hugging Face, beginning with NorwAI-Llama2-7B.

  4. 2025

    Next model planned from Magistral

    In September 2025 the centre said its next, larger model would start from Mistral's 24B Magistral and avoid copyrighted material.

  5. 2026

    Technical report on NorwAI LLMs

    Gulla, Liu and Zhang documented the model suite on arXiv in January 2026.
