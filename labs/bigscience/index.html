About

BigScience was a volunteer-driven research workshop in which several hundred researchers worked in the open, with Hugging Face coordinating and French government funding supporting it.

The main output was BLOOM, released on 12 July 2022 with 176 billion parameters, 46 natural languages and 13 programming languages. It was trained between March and July 2022 on the Jean Zay supercomputer on the ROOTS corpus.

The code is Apache 2.0 and the weights use the BigScience Responsible AI License. Related releases include xP3 and BLOOMZ.

Mission and ambitions

The workshop aimed to study large language models openly, with participants from many countries sharing data, code and evaluation.
