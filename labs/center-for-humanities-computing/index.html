About

The Center for Humanities Computing at Aarhus University was set up by the faculty with Kristoffer Nielbo as its first director, and Nielbo later became the first Danish Professor of Humanities Computing. It runs computational infrastructure for humanities researchers.

Its NLP work includes DaCy, a unified Danish pipeline built on spaCy by Kenneth Enevoldsen, Lasse Hansen and Nielbo, odyCy for Ancient Greek, and the Scandinavian Embedding Benchmark co-created with partners.

The centre's GitHub organization has about 188 repositories and its models serve Nordic NLP researchers and humanities scholars.

Mission and ambitions

The centre seeks to make computational methods available across the humanities and to provide open Danish and Scandinavian language tools.
