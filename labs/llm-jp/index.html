About

LLM-jp is a researcher community organized by the Research and Development Center for Large Language Models at the National Institute of Informatics. NII established the volunteer group in May 2023, and by December 2024 it counted more than 1,900 participants. Sadao Kurohashi leads the center and the effort.

It organizes working groups on corpus construction, model building, evaluation and tuning, safety, dialogue, multimodal and academic domains. The group states goals of developing open state-of-the-art Japanese models, sharing data and compute between organizations, and publishing models, tools and technical materials.

The LLM-jp-3 series, trained on llm-jp-corpus v3, has been released since September 2024 in sizes from 150 million to 172 billion parameters, though access to the 172B pretrained model needs approval and redistribution is restricted.

Mission and ambitions

LLM-jp aims to build open Japanese large language models, share knowledge among researchers and enable cross-organization collaboration on data and compute.

Milestones

  1. 2023

    LLM-jp established

    NII founded the volunteer research community.

  2. 2023

    Model suite v1.0

    First open model suite, with 13B models and fine-tuned variants, released 20 October 2023.

  3. 2024

    Model suite v2.0 and LLM-jp-3

    v2.0 followed on 30 April 2024, and the LLM-jp-3 series was released from September 2024.

  4. 2025

    LLM-jp-3 MoE series

    Extended the family with mixture-of-experts models.
