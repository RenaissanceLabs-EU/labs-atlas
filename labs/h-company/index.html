About

H Company was founded in Paris in 2023 by five DeepMind researchers, and Gautier Cloix became CEO in 2025. It reports raising more than 200 million euros in 2024 from backers including Accel, UiPath, Bpifrance, Bernard Arnault and Eric Schmidt.

In 2025 it launched Holo1, described as the first open-weight vision-language model for computer use, together with the Surfer-H web agent. Holo1.5 and Holo2 followed in 2025 and Holo3 and Holo4 in 2026.

Mission and ambitions

H Company aims to build agents that operate software and the web on a user's behalf, and it shares its Holo models as open weights.
