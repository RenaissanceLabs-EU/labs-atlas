About

Gensyn was founded in 2020 in London by Ben Fielding and Harry Grieve. It describes its mission as building machines that predict the future and works on predictive intelligence infrastructure and verifiable AI.

The company has raised around 50 million dollars, including a 43 million dollar Series A led by a16z crypto in 2023, with backing from CoinFund, Protocol Labs, Canonical Crypto, Eden Block and Maven 11. Its GitHub organisation publishes open-source frameworks, including RL Swarm for collaborative training over the internet.

Mission and ambitions

Gensyn aims to make machine learning compute open and verifiable by coordinating training across a decentralised network.
