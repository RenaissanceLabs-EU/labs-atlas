About

LINAGORA was founded in 2000 by Alexandre Zapolsky and Michel-Marie Maudet, and its headquarters moved to Issy-les-Moulineaux in 2024. Its products include Twake Workplace, OpenPaaS and the LinTO voice assistant, and its GitHub organization holds more than 500 repositories.

In 2023 it created the OpenLLM France community, and in January 2025 it released Lucie, an open-source sovereign large language model. The release drew criticism after tests of an unfinished version and was temporarily suspended.

Mission and ambitions

To invent and develop ethical, open-source technology and to support open sovereign AI in France.
