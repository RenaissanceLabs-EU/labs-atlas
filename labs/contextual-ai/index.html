About

Contextual AI is a company founded in 2023 by Douwe Kiela and Amanpreet Singh, both formerly at Facebook AI Research and Hugging Face. It builds retrieval-augmented generation systems for enterprise use and pairs its commercial platform with research that publishes open code and models.

The company raised a $20 million seed round led by Bain Capital Ventures in June 2023 and an $80 million Series A led by Greycroft in August 2024. Its research includes KTO, an alignment method that needs only a binary signal of whether an output is desirable, and GRIT, which trains one model for both generation and embedding.

Its GitHub and Hugging Face organizations host alignment libraries, retrieval models and an instruction-following reranker released in March 2025, aimed at practitioners who build their own RAG pipelines.

Mission and ambitions

The company works to make language models reliable on enterprise data by grounding them in retrieval. Its open releases are meant to share the underlying alignment and retrieval methods with the research community.
