About

ellamind GmbH works on evaluation, data and environments for AI systems and helps regulated industries such as health insurance, banking and public administration bring them into production. Its products include elluminate, an agentic evaluation platform, and an accessibility testing agent.

The company open-sources base-eval, curated lm-evaluation-harness task configurations for English and German base models, and inference-hive, distributed LLM inference for SLURM clusters, both under Apache-2.0. It also publishes propella-1, a family of small multilingual annotation models, and sui-1, a 24B-parameter summarization model with inline citations, on Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

ellamind focuses on making AI systems measurable and dependable for organisations in regulated sectors, and shares part of its tooling as open source.
