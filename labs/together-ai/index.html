About

Together AI was founded by Vipul Ved Prakash, Ce Zhang, Chris Ré and Percy Liang, with Tri Dao joining as chief scientist in July 2023. It combines a commercial inference cloud with an open research group.

In April 2023 it released RedPajama, a 1.2 trillion token pre-training dataset reproducing the LLaMA recipe, and later the 30 trillion token RedPajama-V2. It announced FlashAttention-2 in July 2023.

Its research outputs are open, but the company's main business is a paid platform.

Mission and ambitions

Together aims to provide an open, high-performance platform for building with open-source models.
