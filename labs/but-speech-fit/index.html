About

BUT Speech@FIT is the speech and language research group at the Faculty of Information Technology, Brno University of Technology. Its GitHub organization dates from 2019.

The group released VBx, a Bayesian HMM clustering approach to speaker diarization described by Landini, Profant, Diez and Burget, and DiariZen, a pipeline built on pyannote with WavLM-based models. Jan Černocký and Lukáš Burget are co-authors on its diarization system papers.

On Hugging Face the group published csmpt7b, a Czech-focused 7B model, in 2024. Its tools serve speech researchers and diarization challenge participants.

Mission and ambitions

The group advances speech processing and shares open recipes and models, especially for diarization and Czech language technology.
