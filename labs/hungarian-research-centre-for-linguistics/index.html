About

The group grew from the Corpus Linguistics Department formed in 1997 and is led by Enikő Héja, with Gábor Prószéky and Tamás Váradi among its senior staff. It builds language resources, tools and neural models for Hungarian.

Its work includes the Hungarian National Corpus, completed in 2005 with a second edition in 2014, the e-magyar text processing system, and the HuLU benchmark modeled on GLUE. Its PULI family includes PULI-GPT-3SX, a seven-billion-parameter Hungarian model.

Mission and ambitions

To develop language technology for Hungarian, from corpora and morphology to modern neural language models.
