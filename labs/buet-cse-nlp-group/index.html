About

The CSE NLP group at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology works on machine translation, multilingual NLP and language understanding. Its faculty include Rifat Shahriyar, M. Sohel Rahman and Anindya Iqbal.

It open-sourced BanglaBERT in 2021 and BanglaT5 in 2022, and published XL-Sum, a 44-language summarisation dataset, at ACL 2021 Findings.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to provide open models and benchmarks for Bangla.
