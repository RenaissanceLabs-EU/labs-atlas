About

The Swiss AI Initiative started in December 2023, seeded with more than 10 million GPU hours on the Alps system run by CSCS and a CHF 20 million grant from the ETH Domain. It is run with the ETH AI Center and the EPFL AI Center, with Swisscom as strategic partner.

Its main output is Apertus, released on 2 September 2025 in 8B and 70B sizes under Apache 2.0. Apertus was trained on over 1,800 languages and designed to follow European copyright law, with an emphasis on transparency and auditability.

An Apertus 1.5 release is listed for July 2026.

Mission and ambitions

The initiative aims to build transparent, sovereign foundation models for Switzerland and to share them with the wider research community.
