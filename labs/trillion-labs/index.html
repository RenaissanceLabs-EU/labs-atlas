About

Trillion Labs was founded in Seoul in 2024 by Jaemin Shin, formerly a research scientist on Naver's HyperCLOVA X. It raised pre-seed funding of 5.8 million dollars in September 2024.

It open-sourced the preview Trillion-7B model in March 2025 and Tri-21B in July 2025, a model trained from scratch that runs on a single GPU. Tri 21B Think, adding reinforcement learning for reasoning, followed in February 2026.

Mission and ambitions

Trillion Labs aims to build efficient Korean-capable models through from-scratch pretraining.
