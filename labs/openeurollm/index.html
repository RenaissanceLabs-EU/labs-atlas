About

OpenEuroLLM is co-funded by the European Union through the Digital Europe Programme. Its consortium of 21 organizations includes 12 universities and research organizations, six companies and three high-performance computing centres.

Press and university sources describe it as coordinated by Jan Hajič of Charles University and co-led by Peter Sarlin of AMD Silo AI, starting in February 2025 for 36 months. The project publishes models, data and evaluation results on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Its goals include strong multilingual models for EU official languages, wider evaluation including AI Act alignment, open datasets, open tools and recipes, and a community of developers. Recent Hugging Face releases include the Prelude9B work and numerous data-mix experiments.

Mission and ambitions

OpenEuroLLM aims to provide transparent, performant models for European languages while preserving linguistic and cultural diversity, and to share the data, code and recipes openly.
