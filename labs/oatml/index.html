About

The Oxford Applied and Theoretical Machine Learning group sits in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford. Yarin Gal announced it in October 2018 and leads it as Associate Professor of Machine Learning.

Its research covers Bayesian deep learning, uncertainty estimation and the safety of machine learning systems. Code accompanying its papers is released on GitHub.

Former members include Sebastian Farquhar, Clare Lyle, Pascal Notin and Joost van Amersfoort.

Mission and ambitions

The group works on principled machine learning methods that quantify uncertainty and make learning systems more reliable.
