About

Proxecto Nós was launched in 2020 by the Xunta de Galicia and entrusted to the University of Santiago de Compostela through CiTIUS and the Instituto da Lingua Galega (ILG). It aims to give Galician openly licensed resources and tools in intelligent technologies.

It produced CorpusNós, described as the largest openly available collection of Galician text, and the first generative language models focused on Galician, including the 1.3 billion parameter Carballo, adapted from Catalan-Spanish-English models of the Aina project. A 2024 arXiv paper describes the models.

The project also operates public demonstrators for machine translation, speech synthesis, speech recognition and retrieval-augmented chat. CiTIUS was later selected to lead the Galician model in a Microsoft collaboration with BSC and HiTZ on models for Spain's official languages.

Mission and ambitions

The project seeks to ensure Galician has the digital resources and AI technology needed for everyday use.

Milestones

  1. 2020

    Proxecto Nós starts

    Launched by the Xunta de Galicia and hosted by CiTIUS and the ILG at USC.

  2. 2022

    Project paper at TDLE

    Presented the project's aims and resources.

  3. 2024

    First Galician LLMs

    Released Carballo models trained on CorpusNós, as described in the arXiv paper.

  4. 2024

    Microsoft collaboration

    CiTIUS selected to lead the Galician model in a multilingual initiative with BSC and HiTZ.

  5. 2025

    Galician evaluation tool presented

    A tool for evaluating Galician in large AI models was presented at the European Parliament.
