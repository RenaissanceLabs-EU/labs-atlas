About

Moonshot AI was founded in Beijing in March 2023 by Yang Zhilin, Zhou Xinyu and Wu Yuxin, who had been schoolfriends at Tsinghua University. Yang previously worked at Google Brain and Meta. The company is named after Pink Floyd's The Dark Side of the Moon.

It operates the Kimi assistant, an API platform and the Kimi Code command-line agent, and publishes model weights on Hugging Face. Kimi K2 appeared in July 2025, K2.5 added native vision in January 2026, K3 followed in mid-2026 and Kimi K3.1 Pro was published in November 2026.

Moonshot is venture-backed, with Alibaba and Tencent among its investors, and releases K3 under a custom license that requires revenue sharing from very large commercial inference providers.

Mission and ambitions

Moonshot states its aim as building foundation models toward artificial general intelligence, sharing research with the open-source community.

Milestones

  1. 2023

    Kimi chatbot

    Moonshot released the first version of Kimi in October 2023, capable of long Chinese-language context.

  2. 2025

    Kimi K2

    The K2 weights were published on Hugging Face in July 2025.

  3. 2026

    Kimi K2.5

    A multimodal upgrade with a 400-million-parameter MoonViT vision encoder and Agent Swarm, released in January 2026.

  4. 2026

    Kimi K3

    A 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model family launched in July 2026.

  5. 2026

    Kimi K3.1 Pro

    A model with a one-million-token context window published in October and November 2026.
