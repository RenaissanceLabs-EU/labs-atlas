About

Moondream is a family of small vision-language models built by M87 Labs. The models handle captioning, querying, detection, pointing and segmentation, and run on GPUs, CPUs and edge hardware.

Moondream 3.1 uses a sparse mixture-of-experts design with 9 billion parameters, 2 billion of them active. The site reports more than five million monthly downloads, open weights and commercial use permitted. The company also offers a hosted API, a fine-tuning product and the Photon inference engine.

In October 2026 Moondream announced that it had been acquired by Prime Intellect, which says the combined team will accelerate open models and fast multimodal AI.

Mission and ambitions

Moondream aims to make capable visual AI small and fast enough to be fine-tuned and deployed on real-world edge cases anywhere.
