About

The Visual Geometry Group is within the Department of Engineering Science at Oxford, founded by Andrew Zisserman.

The 2014 VGG paper by Simonyan and Zisserman placed first in localization and second in classification at ILSVRC 2014.

GitHub hosts tools.

Mission and ambitions

The group advances computer vision and shares data and software.
