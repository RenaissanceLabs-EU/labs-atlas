About

StepFun, formally Shanghai Jieyue Xingchen Intelligent Technology, was founded in April 2023 by Jiang Daxin, Zhu Yibo and Jiao Binxing, all formerly of Microsoft. It is often counted among China's six leading foundation-model startups, and its investors include Tencent and Qiming Venture Partners.

The company builds large language, multimodal, image, video and audio models. Step-2, a trillion-parameter model, appeared in 2024, and it open-sourced Step-Video-T2V and Step-Audio in February 2025 together with Geely.

Its recent open releases, Step 3.5 Flash in February 2026 and Step 3.7 Flash in May 2026, use the Apache 2.0 license. Its latest preview model, Step 5, is closed-weight, so openness varies by release.

Mission and ambitions

StepFun states an aim of building frontier multimodal models and making many of them available to developers.
