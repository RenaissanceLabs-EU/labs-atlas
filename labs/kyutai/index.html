About

Kyutai is an open-science AI research lab in Paris, funded by the Iliad Group, the CMA CGM Group and Schmidt Sciences. Its team is led by Patrick Pérez as chief executive, with Alexandre Défossez as head of research and Laurent Mazaré as head of engineering. Edouard Grave and Hervé Jegou are listed among its alumni.

The lab is known for Moshi, described as the first speech-native dialogue system, which processes speech directly rather than converting it to text and back. It also released Unmute, which gives any text model real-time listening and speaking, and Helium, a multilingual two-billion-parameter language model. Papers, code and weights are published openly.

Its recent research output covers text-to-speech, video autoencoders and world models.

Mission and ambitions

Kyutai aims to advance open research on speech and multimodal AI, publishing models, code and papers for the community.
