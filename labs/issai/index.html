About

The Institute of Smart Systems and Artificial Intelligence (ISSAI) was founded in September 2019 at Nazarbayev University in Astana. It was set up to build national research capacity in artificial intelligence and draws on experience from institutions in Asia, Europe and North America. Its home is the university's campus on Kabanbay Batyr Avenue.

ISSAI is known for open resources for the Kazakh language, including the Kazakh Speech Corpus, whose second edition holds about 1,200 hours of transcribed speech with Kazakh-Russian code-switching, and the KazakhTTS corpora. It worked with the Ministry of Science and Higher Education on KazLLM, whose open-source version is on Hugging Face.

The institute also runs applied projects such as the Tilsync partnership offering and publishes code on GitHub under the IS2AI organization.

Mission and ambitions

ISSAI aims to develop a national capacity for artificial intelligence research in Kazakhstan, with a focus on the Kazakh language and open resources.

Milestones

  1. 2019

    ISSAI founded

    Established at Nazarbayev University to build national AI research capacity.

  2. 2021

    Kazakh Speech Corpus

    Published a crowdsourced open Kazakh speech corpus with baseline recognition results.

  3. 2022

    KazakhTTS2

    Expanded the open Kazakh TTS corpus from 93 to 271 hours with five speakers.

  4. 2022

    KSC2

    Released the second Kazakh Speech Corpus of roughly 1,200 hours with code-switched speech.

  5. 2024

    KazLLM

    Contributed to a Kazakh large language model built with the Ministry, with an open version on Hugging Face.
