About

ARC Prize Foundation is a nonprofit dedicated to accelerating progress toward AGI. It is built around ARC-AGI, the benchmark created by Francois Chollet, and runs open prize competitions on Kaggle.

The foundation is led by co-founders Francois Chollet and Mike Knoop and president Greg Kamradt, who sit on the board with a small team working on benchmark design, engineering and model testing.

Its site lists the ARC Prize 2024 and 2025 competitions and the ARC-AGI-2 and ARC-AGI-3 benchmarks, with ARC Prize 2026 results due on December 4, 2026.

Mission and ambitions

The foundation states that it exists to guide researchers toward more general intelligence by publishing measurable, open benchmarks and prizes.
