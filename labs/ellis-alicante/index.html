About

ELLIS Alicante was founded in 2020 as a foundation hosting a research unit of the ELLIS network in Spain. It resulted from a 2019 bid, led by Nuria Oliver, for Alicante to host an ELLIS unit, and she serves as its director.

Its research addresses modeling human behavior at individual and societal scales, systems that interact naturally with people, and ethical AI covering fairness, privacy and transparency. It is funded by the Generalitat Valenciana, the European Regional Development Fund and partners including Intel and Fundación Banco Sabadell, and reports over 38 million euros in EU project budgets.

Paper code and evaluation repositories are published on GitHub, including tutorials on graph rewiring and fairness, an open framework for social media AR filters, and evaluations of how language models handle mental-health crises.

Mission and ambitions

To advance ethical, responsible and human-centric artificial intelligence that serves society.

Milestones

  1. 2019

    Alicante wins an ELLIS unit

    A bid led by Nuria Oliver brought a unit of the ELLIS network to Alicante.

  2. 2020

    ELLIS Alicante foundation established

    The foundation began operating and opened its GitHub organization in April 2020.

  3. 2022

    OpenFilter framework

    The group released a framework to democratize research access to social media AR filters, with Piera Riccio, Nuria Oliver and coauthors.

  4. 2022

    Graph rewiring tutorial

    A tutorial on graph rewiring and fairness was presented at the Learning on Graphs conference.

  5. 2025

    Multilingual and multimodal red teaming

    Code accompanying a paper on red teaming multimodal models was presented at the ACL 2025 LLM security workshop.

  6. 2025

    LLM mental-health crisis evaluation

    An evaluation of how large language models handle mental-health crises was published with open code.
