About

CompVis is the Computer Vision and Learning group at LMU Munich, formerly the Computer Vision Group at Heidelberg University, led by Prof. Dr. Bjorn Ommer. Its research covers generative visual synthesis, invertible deep models for explainable AI, deep metric and representation learning, and self-supervised learning.

The group is best known for latent diffusion models, presented at CVPR 2022 as a method for high-resolution image synthesis. Source code and models were released on GitHub, and the approach underlies Stable Diffusion.

Newer projects listed on the group's site include SCFlow, TREAD and CleanDIFT. The group also applies its methods to the digital humanities and the life sciences.

Mission and ambitions

The group pursues fundamental research in computer vision and machine learning, with an emphasis on generative models and interpretable representations. It publishes code and models so that others can build on the work.

Milestones

  1. 2021

    Taming Transformers

    Combined convolutional and transformer models for high-resolution synthesis, with code released.

  2. 2022

    Latent diffusion models

    The group presented high-resolution image synthesis with latent diffusion at CVPR and released code and models on GitHub.

  3. 2022

    Stable Diffusion

    The group's latent diffusion work was the foundation for the Stable Diffusion image generator.

  4. 2024

    CleanDIFT

    Released a method for extracting diffusion features without added noise.
