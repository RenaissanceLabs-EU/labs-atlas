About

HPC-AI Tech was founded by Yang You, a professor at the National University of Singapore who earned his PhD at UC Berkeley. The company grew out of research on efficient distributed training and became known for Colossal-AI, an open-source software stack for training and serving large models. It raised a seed round of about six million dollars in 2022 and a 50 million dollar Series A announced in 2024.

Its open-source output includes Colossal-AI and Open-Sora, a video generation project that attracted wide attention on GitHub. Alongside these, the company operates a commercial GPU cloud platform and a model platform with OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic-compatible APIs, and offers enterprise services such as fine-tuning and performance optimization.

The business model combines free open-source infrastructure with paid cloud and services, so the open projects serve as the technical base of the commercial offering.

Mission and ambitions

The company aims to lower the cost and complexity of training and running large AI models, and to keep the core tooling open while selling cloud and enterprise services on top.
