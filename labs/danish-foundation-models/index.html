About

Danish Foundation Models is a collaborative effort across Danish universities and research institutions to develop, evaluate and adapt open language AI for Danish society. The core partners are the University of Southern Denmark, Aarhus University through its Center for Humanities Computing, the University of Copenhagen and the Alexandra Institute.

Its members include professors Bolette Sandford Pedersen at Copenhagen, Kristoffer Nielbo at Aarhus and Peter Schneider-Kamp at Southern Denmark, with Kenneth Enevoldsen and many postdoctoral and PhD researchers. The project argues that smaller languages risk exclusion as language models become infrastructure, and it publishes models and datasets openly on Hugging Face.

The 2023 paper Danish Foundation Models set out the program. Danish Dynaword, released in 2025, is an openly licensed, continuously updated collection of Danish text derived from the public parts of the Danish Gigaword, with a companion post-training dataset, dfm-dyna-instruct.

Mission and ambitions

The consortium aims to give Danish society open, well-evaluated language technology that it can adopt and adapt. It treats openness of models and data as a condition for public benefit.
