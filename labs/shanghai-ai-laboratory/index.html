About

Shanghai AI Laboratory is a non-profit research institution inaugurated at the World AI Conference in Shanghai in 2020. Zhou Bowen became director, appearing in that role in July 2024.

The lab's Intern series includes InternLM multilingual foundation and chat models, InternVL multimodal models, and Intern-S1, a 241B-parameter mixture-of-experts scientific multimodal model with 28B parameters active per token, released under Apache 2.0.

It also open-sources a toolchain around these models.

Mission and ambitions

The lab aims to advance open general AI, with an emphasis on science.
