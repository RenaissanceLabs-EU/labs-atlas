About

Aleph Alpha was founded in 2019 in Heidelberg by Jonas Andrulis and Samuel Weinbach. It raised a 5.3 million euro seed round in 2020, a further 23 million euros in 2021, and in November 2023 announced a large round led by Schwarz Gruppe and the Dieter Schwarz Foundation.

The company sells specialised sovereign language models and solutions to organisations, and also publishes research code, datasets and models. Its Hugging Face organization carries the Kolibri model, a German and English mixture-of-experts model released in October 2026 under Apache 2.0.

In April 2026 Cohere announced that it would acquire Aleph Alpha, and a definitive merger agreement followed in September 2026. The combined company will operate as Cohere, with Heidelberg kept as a research centre, subject to regulatory approval.

Mission and ambitions

The company says it builds specialised sovereign models that give customers control over their data and adapt to their context. Its open releases support German-language and tokenizer research.
