About

Bioptimus is a French company that describes its goal as a world model of biology. Its platforms are H-Optimus for pathology, M-Optimus for multimodal data and STELA, a multi-institutional data initiative.

It announced H-Optimus-0 in July 2024, a 1.1-billion-parameter pathology model described as open source. It raised a 35 million dollar seed round led by Sofinnova Partners and, in January 2025, 41 million dollars led by Cathay Innovation.

Mission and ambitions

Bioptimus says it aims to build a world model of biology across modalities and scales.
