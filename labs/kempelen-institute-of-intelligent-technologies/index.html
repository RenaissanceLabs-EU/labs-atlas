About

The Kempelen Institute of Intelligent Technologies (KInIT) is an independent, non-profit institute in Bratislava. It was set up with backing from Slovak companies including ESET, Tatra banka, Innovatrics and Softec, and aims to connect the private and public sectors with multidisciplinary research. Mária Bieliková is Director General.

Its research areas include web and user data processing and disinformation, natural language processing, data analysis for green energy, information security, and ethics of intelligent technologies. In 2021 its NLP team consulted on and evaluated SlovakBERT, the first public large neural language model for Slovak, trained with partner Gerulata Technologies.

The institute publishes Slovak models and benchmarks, including the 2025 skLEP benchmark, on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Mission and ambitions

KInIT states that it aims to improve Slovakia's competitiveness through multidisciplinary research on intelligent technologies, connect companies and public bodies, and encourage responsible innovation and talent development.
