About

Transluce was co-founded in 2024 by Jacob Steinhardt and Sarah Schwettmann, with Steinhardt on leave from UC Berkeley and Schwettmann previously at MIT CSAIL. It describes itself as a nonprofit that builds open, scalable tools for understanding frontier AI.

It launched publicly on 23 October 2024 with neuron-description tools, Monitor and investigator agents. It later released Docent, which analyzes agent transcripts to find bugs and unexpected behaviour and is used by more than 25 organizations.

Mission and ambitions

Transluce aims to enable scalable, democratic oversight of AI through open tooling.
