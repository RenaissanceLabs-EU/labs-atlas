About

The AI-lab at the National Library of Norway explores how AI can serve libraries, archives and museums, and builds datasets and models for the wider community. It is a unit of the library rather than a separate company.

Its first major output, the Norwegian Transformer Model Project of 2020 to 2021, produced nb-bert-base, described as the first working Norwegian transformer model, and the Norwegian Colossal Corpus. A later Norwegian Speech Transformer Project, led by Per Egil Kummervold from September 2022, targeted better Norwegian speech-to-text.

The lab publishes its work through its GitHub and Hugging Face organizations.

Mission and ambitions

The lab pursues applications of AI in libraries, archives and museums and contributes datasets and models so that the library can help shape good AI for Norwegian.
