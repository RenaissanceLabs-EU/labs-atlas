About

Illuin Technology is a Paris-based AI company that publishes research code under the illuin-tech GitHub organization and models on Hugging Face. Its researchers work with academic partners including CentraleSupélec and EPFL.

In February 2024 the team co-authored CroissantLLM, a 1.3B-parameter bilingual French-English model with open training data. In June 2024 it released ColPali, which retrieves documents using vision-language models, together with the ViDoRe benchmark, and in 2025 it followed with the ModernVBERT encoder under the MIT license.

Mission and ambitions

To build practical AI systems and advance open research on document understanding and retrieval.

  2. 2024

    Faysse and colleagues introduced efficient document retrieval with vision language models.

  3. 2024

    Evaluation code for visual document retrieval was released alongside ColPali.

  4. 2025

    Contextual embeddings

    A model implementation for contextual embeddings was published.

  5. 2025

    ModernVBERT

    A 250M-parameter vision-language encoder for document retrieval was released under the MIT license.
