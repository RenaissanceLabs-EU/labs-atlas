About

FAR.AI describes itself as an AI security research and adoption non-profit. It was co-founded by Adam Gleave, who is CEO, and Karl Berzins, who is president.

Its work spans in-house research agendas, applied research that validates ideas through near-frontier-scale prototypes, pre-deployment testing and consulting with frontier developers, government capacity building, field-building events and grantmaking, and public safety evaluations. Its GitHub organisation, AlignmentResearch, publishes code from this work.

Mission and ambitions

FAR.AI aims to close the gap between safety research and real-world adoption so that advanced AI is trustworthy, secure and beneficial.
