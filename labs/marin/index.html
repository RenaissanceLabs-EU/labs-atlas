About

Marin is an open lab started in 2024 at Stanford University by David Hall and Percy Liang. It is primarily developed by Open Athena with community contributors, and it releases its discussions, code, model weights, data sources, successful and failed experiments and tools for learning.

The lab released an 8B model in May 2025 and a 32B model in October 2025. Google's open source team reported that Marin 32B beat Olmo 2 32B on 32 of 42 tasks, and the model is released under Apache 2.0. Later work covers mixture-of-experts training, a scaling suite called Delphi, a 25-trillion-token data pipeline and post-training data.

Everything is documented in public pages so others can reproduce and extend runs.

Mission and ambitions

Marin aims to develop foundation models in the open, sharing every step from data to weights so the community can learn from and build on them.
