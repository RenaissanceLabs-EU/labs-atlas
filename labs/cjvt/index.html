About

CJVT, the Centre for Language Resources and Technologies, is a research unit of the University of Ljubljana with its organizational base at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science and five other participating faculties. It develops and maintains digital language resources for contemporary Slovene and makes tools available free of charge.

Its resources include the Sloleks morphological lexicon, which invites users to vote on accentuation and pronunciation, the ssj500k training corpus and its successor SUK, and the Tolmac system for translating lectures from Slovene. The centre joined the ELEXIS lexicography network in 2020 and works within a national program on language resources and technologies for Slovene that runs from 2019 to 2027.

The GaMS (Generative Model for Slovene) family began in 2024 with a 1 billion parameter model and has grown to 2B, 9B and 27B releases built on Gemma. A March 2026 preprint presents GaMS3-12B, adapted from Gemma 3 with continual pretraining on about 140 billion tokens and an open OCR pipeline for digitizing older Slovene texts.

Mission and ambitions

CJVT aims to build a long-term infrastructure of Slovene language resources and technology and to keep its tools and data openly available.

Milestones

  1. 2019

    National language technology program

    CJVT began work in the national program on language resources and technologies for Slovene, running from 2019 to 2027.

  2. 2020

    ELEXIS membership

    The centre joined the European lexicography infrastructure network.

  3. 2024

    GaMS 1B

    Published the first Generative Model for Slovene with 1 billion parameters (arXiv 2410.06898).

  4. 2026

    GaMS3-12B

    Presented a 12 billion parameter Slovene model adapted from Gemma 3, with an open OCR pipeline.
