About

Hooshvare Research Lab is a Tehran-based group that publishes open models and datasets for Persian natural language processing. Its GitHub organization describes it as a platform for sharing information about artificial intelligence concepts with enthusiasts. The lab's work is published through the Hooshvare organizations on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Its best-known release is ParsBERT, a monolingual BERT-style model for Persian described in a May 2020 preprint by Mehrdad Farahani, Mohammad Gharachorloo, Marzieh Farahani and Mohammad Manthouri. The paper reports gains over multilingual BERT on sentiment analysis, text classification and named entity recognition. The lab later added a Persian GPT-2 model, NER models, a news summarization dataset and workshop materials.

Code and weights are released under permissive licenses such as MIT and Apache 2.0, which has made the models a common baseline for Persian-language research and applications.

Mission and ambitions

The lab aims to make capable language models and datasets for Persian freely available, so that researchers and developers working in the language are not limited to multilingual models built for other languages.

Milestones

  1. 2020

    ParsBERT preprint

    Introduced a Persian-specific BERT-Base model that outperformed multilingual BERT on three downstream tasks.

  2. 2020

    pn-summary dataset

    Released a Persian news summarization dataset alongside ParsBERT-based summarization work.

  3. 2021

    ParsBERT in Neural Processing Letters

    Peer-reviewed journal version of the ParsBERT work published in volume 53.

  4. 2021

    Persian GPT-2 and NER models

    Published ParsGPT and ParsNER repositories to extend the Persian model family beyond encoders.

  5. 2021

    ParsBERT workshop

    Published workshop material covering ParsBERT's architecture and applications.
