About

Neuronpedia was created by Johnny Lin, an engineer who previously worked at Apple. It is an open-source platform where researchers and the public can search the features inside language models, steer model behavior and trace circuits.

It hosts feature dashboards and tooling for releases such as Gemma Scope 2, Llama Scope and SAE Bench, and recent additions include Circuit Tracer, the Assistant Axis, Natural Language Autoencoders and Jacobian Lens. It also offers an API and a community Slack.

Funding comes from Decode Research, Open Philanthropy, the Long Term Future Fund and others, and its users include groups at Anthropic, Google DeepMind, EleutherAI and Apollo Research.

Mission and ambitions

Neuronpedia aims to make model internals easy to inspect and share, so that interpretability research is open and collaborative.
