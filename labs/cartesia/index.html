About

Cartesia was founded in 2023 by Karan Goel, Albert Gu, Arjun Desai and Brandon Yang, researchers who worked on state space models in Chris Re's group at Stanford. Goel serves as chief executive and Gu, a lead author of Mamba and a professor at Carnegie Mellon University, serves as chief scientist.

The company builds real-time voice systems on state-space architectures, which process sequences with a fixed-size state rather than attention over the whole context. It raised a $22 million seed round led by Index Ventures, bringing its total to $27 million, and a $64 million Series A led by Kleiner Perkins in March 2025.

Alongside its commercial speech products, Cartesia publishes open research artifacts on Hugging Face and GitHub. These include the Rene language model and the Llamba family of distilled recurrent models, described in a February 2025 paper that converts Llama models into recurrent form with a small fraction of the usual training data.

Mission and ambitions

Cartesia aims to build real-time, on-device voice AI on state-space model architectures, and shares part of its model research openly.
