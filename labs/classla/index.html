About

CLASSLA was recognised by CLARIN on 19 March 2019. It is operated by CLARIN.SI, the Institute of Croatian Language and CLADA-BG, with hosting at the Jožef Stefan Institute in Ljubljana.

It offers a helpdesk, the CLASSLA-Stanza pipeline for South Slavic languages, with an improved version released in September 2023, and the largest general web corpora for these languages. BERTić, a model for BCMS languages, is described in arXiv 2104.09243.

Recent work includes benchmarks and an LLM evaluation dashboard for South Slavic languages.

Mission and ambitions

The centre provides expertise and open resources for processing South Slavic languages and supports researchers across the region.
