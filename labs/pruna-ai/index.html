About

Pruna AI was founded in 2023 by Bertrand Charpentier, Rayan Nait Mazi, John Rachwan and Stephan Günnemann, researchers in AI efficiency with ties to the Technical University of Munich. It operates research hubs in Munich and Paris.

In November 2024 it announced a 6.5 million dollar seed round led by EQT Ventures, with Daphni, Motier and Kima Ventures participating. In March 2025 it open-sourced its optimization framework, which combines caching, pruning, quantization and distillation and checks quality loss.

The pruna package is MIT-licensed, a paid tier adds further methods, and the company publishes many compressed models on Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

Pruna aims to make AI models smaller, faster and cheaper to run.
