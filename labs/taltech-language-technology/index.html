About

The Laboratory of Language Technology at Tallinn University of Technology develops speech and language technology for Estonian. Tanel Alumae leads related research.

Its general-purpose Estonian ASR is a Whisper-large-v2 fine-tune on about 1200 hours, reporting about 12 percent word error rate on Common Voice 11.

A Whisper fine-tune for Estonian-English-Russian speech translation was published in 2024, and children's speech recognition work in 2025.

Mission and ambitions

The lab builds practical Estonian speech technology and shares models openly.
