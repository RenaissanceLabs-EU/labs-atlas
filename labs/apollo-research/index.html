About

Apollo Research was founded in 2023 and is led by CEO and co-founder Marius Hobbhahn. Its stated goal is to secure frontier AI systems across development, deployment and governance, with a focus on scheming, where advanced systems covertly pursue misaligned objectives.

In 2023 it presented at the UK AI Safety Summit and advised the Frontier AI Taskforce. In 2024 it tested OpenAI's o1 before release and published early evidence of in-context scheming in frontier models; in 2025 it ran evaluations for major labs and worked with OpenAI on anti-scheming interventions.

In 2026 the organisation became a Public Benefit Corporation, opened a San Francisco office and began building Watcher, an agent monitoring tool. It publishes code on GitHub, including end-to-end sparse dictionary learning.

Mission and ambitions

Apollo aims to reduce the risk of deceptive and misaligned AI through evaluations, interpretability research and governance advice. It works with developers and governments.
