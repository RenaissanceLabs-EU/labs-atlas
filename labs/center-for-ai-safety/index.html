About

The Center for AI Safety was started in 2022 and is led by Dan Hendrycks as executive and research director, with Oliver Zhang as managing director. Its stated purpose is to reduce societal-scale risks from artificial intelligence, and it organizes its staff into research, cloud and DevOps, projects and operations teams.

CAIS has operated a compute cluster since March 2023 that gives university safety researchers access to large models, and it has run machine learning safety workshops, a Trojan detection competition and a philosophy fellowship. In May 2023 it published the Statement on AI Risk, signed by more than 600 researchers and public figures.

Its research output includes widely used open evaluations. HarmBench standardizes tests of automated red teaming and refusal, WMDP measures hazardous knowledge and evaluates unlearning methods, and Humanity's Last Exam collects expert-written questions to measure frontier capability.

Mission and ambitions

CAIS works to reduce societal-scale risks from AI through technical research, field-building and advocacy.
