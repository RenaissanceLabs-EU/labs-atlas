About

OpenThoughts is a collaboration of researchers and engineers from universities including Stanford, UC Berkeley, UT Austin, NYU, the University of Washington and TUM, together with LAION. It launched publicly in January 2025 with the aim of producing open post-training data for reasoning models.

The project publishes datasets, training code and models together. Its OpenThoughts3 recipe, described in the paper "OpenThoughts: Data Recipes for Reasoning Models", was derived from more than 1,000 controlled experiments and produced the 1.2 million example OpenThoughts3 dataset and the OpenThinker3-7B model.

Compute and support come from academic centers such as the Jülich Supercomputing Centre, TACC and TU Dresden, as well as industry partners. From late 2025 the group extended its work to agentic tasks under the OpenThoughts-Agent project.

Mission and ambitions

OpenThoughts aims to make the data side of reasoning models as open as the weights, so that the community can study which data choices matter. It treats systematic, reproducible ablation of data pipelines as its main method.

Milestones

  1. 2025

    Project launch

    The OpenThoughts project was announced with the goal of open reasoning data.

  2. 2025

    OpenThinker-32B

    Released an open-data 32B reasoning model, described by the project as scaling up open reasoning.

  3. 2025

    OpenThinker2

    Published a follow-up model family reported to outperform DeepSeek-R1-Distill-32B on its benchmarks.

  4. 2025

    OpenThoughts3

    Released the 1.2M example dataset and a recipe based on over 1,000 experiments, with OpenThinker3-7B.

  5. 2025

    OpenThoughts-Agent

    Launched a project on data recipes and benchmarks for terminal and agentic tasks, followed by the OpenThoughts-TBLite benchmark in 2026.
