About

Nous Research began in 2022 as a group of volunteers who met on Discord, GitHub and Twitter, and became a company in 2023. Reported co-founders include Jeffrey Quesnelle, Karan Malhotra, Teknium and Shivani Mitra, and Bowen Peng is also described as a co-founder.

The group first fine-tuned open models into the Hermes series and published YaRN, a context-extension method. It later released the DisTrO optimizer and the Psyche network for training over the public internet, and in February 2026 launched Hermes Agent, an open-source agent harness.

Funding includes a 50 million dollar Series A led by Paradigm in April 2025 and a 90 million dollar round announced in October 2026.

Mission and ambitions

Nous states its mission as creating and spreading open access to intelligence, so that powerful AI is in the hands of the many.
