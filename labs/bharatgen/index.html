About

BharatGen is a Section 8 not-for-profit company, the BharatGen Technology Foundation, anchored at IIT Bombay and drawing on nine consortium institutions. It is backed by India's Department of Science and Technology and the IndiaAI Mission.

A blog post dates its launch to October 2024. Its models include Param2, a 17B text model, the Sooktam text-to-speech and Shrutam speech-to-text families, and the Patram vision-language models.

Mission and ambitions

Its stated slogan is built from scratch, open weights, made for India.
