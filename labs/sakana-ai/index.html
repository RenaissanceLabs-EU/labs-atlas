About

Sakana AI was founded in 2023 by David Ha (CEO), Llion Jones (CTO) and Ren Ito (COO). Ha and Jones had worked at Google, where they met in Tokyo.

The lab pursues nature-inspired methods. Evolutionary Model Merge combines layers and weights of existing models without retraining, and The AI Scientist is a framework for automated scientific discovery.

It releases research code, models and Japanese datasets.

Mission and ambitions

Sakana AI aims to develop foundation models inspired by natural processes such as evolution and collective intelligence.
