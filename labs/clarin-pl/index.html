About

CLARIN-PL is the Polish node of the CLARIN research infrastructure, based at Wrocław University of Science and Technology. Its GitHub organization dates from 2015.

Its repository hosts plWordNet, led by Maciej Piasecki, with versions 3.0 and 4.0 reaching hundreds of thousands of senses and a licence allowing free use. It also provides the KPWr corpus, the WordnetLoom editor, embeddings tools and the LEPISZCZE benchmark.

The resources serve linguists, humanities scholars and NLP developers working on Polish.

Mission and ambitions

CLARIN-PL provides language resources and tools for Polish humanities and social science research and shares them through an open repository.
