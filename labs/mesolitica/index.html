About

Mesolitica was founded in Kuala Lumpur by Khalil Nooh, chief executive, and Husein Zolkepli, chief technology officer. The team traces the work behind MaLLaM to January 2019, and its GitHub organization dates from February 2019.

It launched MaLLaM, a Malaysian language model trained on nearly 200 billion tokens of Malay content, in December 2023. In January 2024 it published papers on MaLLaM and on Malaysian Mistral, a Mistral 7B model finetuned on Malaysian data, and released models on Hugging Face. It also works on speech systems and voice agents.

The company says it runs operations within Malaysia and open-sources its datasets on GitHub while allowing owners to request removal of their content.

Mission and ambitions

Mesolitica aims to bring AI to Bahasa Melayu speakers and promote AI sovereignty by working entirely within Malaysia.
