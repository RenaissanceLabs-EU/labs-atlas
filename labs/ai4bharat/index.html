About

AI4Bharat is a research lab at IIT Madras that releases most of its work as open source. Its focus areas are transliteration, language understanding and generation, machine translation, speech recognition and speech synthesis.

Its releases include IndicBERT and IndicBART, the IndicTrans2 translation models covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages and English, speech models such as IndicWav2Vec and IndicWhisper, and datasets including Sangraha and IndicVoices.

IndicVoices, funded by the Bhashini initiative of India's IT ministry, provides about 7,300 hours of speech across 22 languages.

Mission and ambitions

AI4Bharat works to give speakers of Indian languages open language technology comparable to what exists for English.

Milestones

  1. 2022

    Nilekani Centre launched

    IIT Madras launched the Nilekani Centre at AI4Bharat in July 2022, funded by a Rs 36 crore grant from Nilekani Philanthropies.

  2. 2023

    IndicTrans2 paper published

    Open translation models for the 22 scheduled Indian languages, with permissive licences.

  3. 2024

    IndicVoices released

    About 7,300 hours of speech from over 16,000 speakers, used to build IndicASR.
