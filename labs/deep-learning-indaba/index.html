About

Deep Learning Indaba was conceived to make Africans active shapers and owners of advances in artificial intelligence. The first Indaba ran from 10 to 15 September 2017 at the University of the Witwatersrand in Johannesburg with about 330 delegates, organized by a group that included Shakir Mohamed, Nyalleng Moorosi, Benjamin Rosman, Ulrich Paquet and Vukosi Marivate.

The organization is led by Indaba Abantu, a steering group of Africans across the continent and abroad, with an advisory board and a board of trustees. Its legal entity is Deep Learning Indaba Ltd, a non-profit company limited by guarantee registered in England and Wales. Mohamed, a DeepMind research director, is listed as co-founder and chair of the trustees.

Programs include the annual Indaba, locally organized IndabaX events, a mentorship program and awards. After Johannesburg in 2017 the gathering moved to Stellenbosch in 2018, and it later took place in Accra in 2023. The 2026 edition is planned for Nigeria, and the GitHub organization publishes open practical notebooks from each Indaba.

Mission and ambitions

The Indaba aims to strengthen machine learning and artificial intelligence in Africa and to raise African participation and diversity in the field.
