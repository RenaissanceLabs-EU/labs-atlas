About

CRFM is a Stanford HAI center directed by Percy Liang. It studies foundation models and builds open tools for evaluating them.

HELM, published in 2022, benchmarked 30 models on 42 scenarios and 7 metrics under standard conditions, and is maintained as a living benchmark. The center also produced Alpaca, a fine-tuned LLaMA 7B model.

It releases BioMedLM and training code on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Mission and ambitions

CRFM aims to make foundation models more transparent and understood through open evaluation and research.
