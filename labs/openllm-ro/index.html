About

OpenLLM-Ro is a community building open large language models for Romanian. Its GitHub organization was created in April 2024, and its first Romanian models, fine-tuned from Llama 2, appeared on 14 May 2024.

It has since released models based on Mistral, Gemma and Llama 3, instruction-tuned and human-aligned variants, training code, evaluation code and two LLM-as-a-judge benchmarks, RoMTBench and RoCulturaBench. In 2025 and 2026 it added pretraining data with a quality classifier, vision-language data and a quantized RoLlama-3.2-1B.

The group has published preprints on its models, data quality and vision-language work, and held a workshop on LLMs for Romanian in September 2024.

Mission and ambitions

OpenLLM-Ro aims to build and share open language models and resources for Romanian.

Milestones

  1. 2024

    First Romanian models

    RoLlama2 models were released on 14 May 2024.

  2. 2024

    RoMistral, RoGemma and RoLlama3

    Additional model families were released between May and June 2024.

  3. 2024

    Training code and benchmarks

    Training code (August 2024) and RoMTBench with RoCulturaBench (August 2024) were published.

  4. 2025

    Pretraining data and quality classifier

    Augmented Romanian pretraining data was released in November 2025.

  5. 2026

    Vision-language resources

    Code, training and evaluation data for Romanian vision-language models were released in June 2026.
