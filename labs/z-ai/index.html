About

Zhipu AI is a Tsinghua University spinoff that rebranded as Z.ai in 2025. Co-founder Tang Jie is chief scientist, and the company listed in Hong Kong on 8 January 2026.

It released GLM-5, a 744B open-weight model under MIT, in February 2026, and later GLM-5.2.

Mission and ambitions

Z.ai aims to build frontier general models and release open weights.
