About

RWKV started as a project by independent researcher Bo Peng, who leads it, and grew into a community project under the Linux Foundation AI & Data.

The architecture has gone through versions named Dove, Eagle, Finch and Goose. RWKV-7 Goose, described in a March 2025 arXiv paper, generalizes the delta rule with vector-valued gating and supports state tracking. Models of 0.4B, 1.5B and 2.9B were released under Apache 2.0.

Contributors come from EleutherAI, Recursal AI and several universities.

Mission and ambitions

RWKV aims to offer an efficient alternative to transformers with linear-time inference.
