About

ALMAnaCH is an Inria team in Paris working on natural language processing and digital humanities. Its members include Benoît Sagot and Djamé Seddah, who are authors of the original CamemBERT paper alongside colleagues from Inria and Sorbonne Université.

CamemBERT, a RoBERTa-based French model released under an MIT licence in 2019, became a widely adopted reference. CamemBERT 2.0 followed in 2024, and CamemBERTa, by Wissam Antoun, Sagot and Seddah, explored data-efficient training. The team's Hugging Face organization lists 92 models and 43 datasets, including biomedical encoders and Gaperon sparse autoencoders.

The models serve researchers, companies and public bodies that need open French and biomedical text processing.

Mission and ambitions

The team builds and shares language models and data for French and historical or specialised text. It treats open release as part of academic practice.
