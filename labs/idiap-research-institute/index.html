About

Idiap Research Institute was created in 1991 as an autonomous non-profit foundation in Martigny, Switzerland, at the initiative of the Dalle Molle Foundation. Its founding partners include the City of Martigny, the Canton of Valais, EPFL, the University of Geneva and Swisscom. The Swiss Confederation recognizes it as an institute of national importance.

The institute researches speech and audio processing, computer vision, biometrics and machine learning under the tagline Artificial Intelligence for Society. In 2002 researchers there developed Torch, an early machine learning library that is a forerunner of PyTorch. Idiap also coordinated the national research center on interactive multimodal information management from 2001 to 2013, spun out companies such as Klewel in 2007, and has become a certified biometric testing center.

The institute publishes a large amount of open-source code and datasets on GitHub and Hugging Face. As of 2026 it is led by an interim management team while it recruits a new director.

Mission and ambitions

Idiap aims to advance research, innovation and education in artificial intelligence for the benefit of society, while remaining a non-profit and independent institute.
