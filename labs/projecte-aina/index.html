About

Projecte Aina is a Catalan government initiative created by the Generalitat de Catalunya's digital policies department to ensure that Catalan is well supported in language technology. The Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC) carries out the technical work, with its Language Technologies Unit credited as author of the models.

The project has built a large Catalan text corpus, started a Catalan speech corpus and published models and datasets on Hugging Face under permissive licenses. Its Salamandra family of 2B, 7B and 40B models, released under Apache 2.0, was trained on the MareNostrum 5 supercomputer with Aina funding.

Resources are intended for reuse by companies, public bodies and researchers, and the project also runs community programs such as the Aina Challenge.

Mission and ambitions

Aina aims to ensure that technology understands and speaks Catalan, with open resources that others can reuse.

Milestones

  1. 2022

    Project begins

    BSC starts building Catalan corpora and models for the Generalitat de Catalunya.

  2. 2023

    Catalan models on Hugging Face

    Open Catalan language, speech and translation models and datasets are published under the projecte-aina organization.

  3. 2024

    Salamandra

    Released 2B, 7B and 40B multilingual models under Apache 2.0, with Catalan upsampled in the training data.

  4. 2025

    Aina Challenge and territorial intelligence programs

    Programs run in parallel with the model work to encourage reuse of the resources.
