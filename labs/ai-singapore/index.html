About

AI Singapore was launched in May 2017 as a national initiative that brings together Singapore's research institutions, start-ups and companies. Its work covers applied research, AI governance, innovation support for companies, talent development and open-source AI products.

Among its products is SEA-LION, a family of open language models for Southeast Asian languages and cultures. Reporting describes a first release in 2023 trained on 11 languages, a version 3 built on Llama and Gemma 2, and a version 4 built on Gemma 3 and on Qwen3 with technical support from Alibaba.

The programme also runs talent schemes such as the AI Apprenticeship Programme and offers courses through LearnAI.

Mission and ambitions

AI Singapore states that it aims to strengthen national AI capabilities, build tools and train talent, and to raise Singapore's profile in AI.
