About

Cardiff NLP is a research group at Cardiff University whose GitHub organization was created in October 2020. Jose Camacho-Collados is a prominent member and an author on its flagship benchmark.

TweetEval, presented in Findings of EMNLP 2020, standardises evaluation across tweet classification tasks. The group also released XLM-T and TimeLMs, and the TweetNLP Python platform in 2022, with Twitter-RoBERTa models trained on tens of millions of tweets.

Its models are widely downloaded from Hugging Face and serve researchers and practitioners analysing social media.

Mission and ambitions

The group aims to build language models and evaluation resources that handle the informal, fast-changing language of social media, and to release them openly.
