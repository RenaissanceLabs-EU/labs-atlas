About

lang-uk is an informal community of programmers, linguists and researchers that supports existing projects and starts new ones to collect Ukrainian text corpora and other language data. Its work is hosted in the lang-uk GitHub organization and on Hugging Face, and the community is open to new members. Ukrainian media first covered its results after its first year of work in 2015.

Co-founder Vsevolod Dyomkin has described the project's main task as collecting and maintaining data, models and tools for processing Ukrainian. Mariana Romanyshyn led a group of linguists who began collecting open Ukrainian text data, and Dmytro Chaplynskyi is credited on the UberText 2.0 corpus paper. The Ukrainian Catholic University and the Kyiv School of Economics are listed as partners.

Its resources serve scientists, entrepreneurs and journalists, covering services, models, libraries, dictionaries, gazetteers, corpora and datasets.

Mission and ambitions

The community aims to create the conditions for full processing of Ukrainian-language text by gathering and openly sharing corpora, tools and models.
