About

Explosion is a Berlin company founded by Matthew Honnibal, its CTO, and Ines Montani, its CEO. It makes spaCy, an open-source library for natural language processing, and Prodigy, an annotation tool, alongside newer products such as Ellf, an assistant for agentic NLP development.

The company maintains its software in the open and describes an employee pool with a stake in the business. Its Hugging Face and GitHub organisations host spaCy models, Thinc and related libraries.

Mission and ambitions

Explosion builds practical developer tools for natural language processing and keeps the core libraries open source.
