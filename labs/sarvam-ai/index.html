About

Sarvam AI was founded in July 2023 in Bengaluru by Vivek Raghavan and Pratyush Kumar, both previously involved with AI4Bharat. Raghavan's background includes India's Aadhaar digital identity system, and Kumar led AI4Bharat.

In December 2023 it announced a 41 million dollar Series A led by Lightspeed, with Peak XV Partners and Khosla Ventures. In February 2026 it launched Sarvam 30B and 105B at the India AI Impact Summit, with weights under Apache 2.0.

The company says the models were trained from scratch on 16T and 12T tokens respectively and target Indian languages. Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis place the 105B below several peer open models. Sarvam also builds speech and enterprise products.

Mission and ambitions

Sarvam aims to build full-stack, sovereign AI for India, including open models.
