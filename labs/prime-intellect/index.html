About

Prime Intellect was co-founded by Vincent Weisser and Johannes Hagemann. Its 2024 launch post framed the company around open, decentralized AI development.

In late 2024 it released INTELLECT-1, a 10B-class model trained on 1 trillion tokens across nodes on three continents with its PRIME distributed training framework, together with data and checkpoints. The company now offers inference, GPU compute, sandboxes and a model factory.

Its open-source projects include prime-rl, a framework for asynchronous reinforcement learning at scale, and the Verifiers library with an Environments Hub that lists thousands of community environments.

Mission and ambitions

Prime Intellect pursues open, globally distributed AI development and wants individuals and small teams to be able to train and own capable models.
