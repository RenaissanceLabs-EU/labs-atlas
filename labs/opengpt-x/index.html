About

OpenGPT-X was a collaborative project funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action from January 2022 to December 2024. It brought together partners from industry, research and media, with Fraunhofer IAIS and IIS leading and compute from the Jülich Supercomputing Centre.

Its main output was Teuken-7B, a seven-billion-parameter model trained from scratch on the 24 official EU languages, with about half of its pretraining data not in English. Instruct and base versions appeared on Hugging Face from September 2024, and the project issued its public press release on 26 November 2024.

The team also published a European LLM Leaderboard in July 2024 and an LLM Workbook, and Deutsche Telekom's T-Systems became the first provider to move Teuken from research to commercial use.

Mission and ambitions

OpenGPT-X set out to develop versatile, trustworthy, multilingual and open language models made in Germany and to support their adoption in the German economy.

Milestones

  1. 2022

    Project start

    The consortium began work in January 2022 with federal funding.

  2. 2024

    European LLM Leaderboard

    The team published a leaderboard for European-language model evaluation in July 2024.

  3. 2024

    Teuken-7B research releases

    Instruct versions v0.4 appeared on Hugging Face in September and October 2024.

  4. 2024

    Teuken-7B public release

    Fraunhofer announced the open release of Teuken-7B on 26 November 2024.

  5. 2025

    Teuken-7B v0.6

    Updated base and instruct versions published between December 2024 and July 2025.
