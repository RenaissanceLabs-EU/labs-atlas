About

The Computational Linguistics and Text Mining Lab at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam is led by Piek Vossen and Antske Fokkens. It has moved from computational lexicology and terminology toward broader work in AI and computational linguistics, with research themes in identity, reference and perspectives, meaning representation, and large language models and interpretability.

Vossen founded the Global WordNet Association in 2000 and presided over it until 2024, and the lab contributes to the Dutch Global Wordnet Grid. It states that it publishes models, software and resources as open source, and also teaches a Language and AI master's program.

MedRoBERTa.nl, introduced in 2021 by Stella Verkijk and Vossen, was pre-trained on nearly 10 million hospital notes from Amsterdam UMC. The lab's Python for Text Analysis course, created in 2016, has over 450 GitHub stars.

Mission and ambitions

The lab combines computational linguistics and text mining and releases its models, software and teaching resources openly.
