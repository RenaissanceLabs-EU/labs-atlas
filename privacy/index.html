Renaissance Labs privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Renaissance Labs handles information in the Renaissance Labs iOS app and on the Open Labs Atlas website.

Information on your device

The app works without an account. It stores your bookmarks, lesson progress and last opened lesson in the app’s settings on your device. The app does not send those settings to Renaissance Labs. They stay on your device until you remove a bookmark, change your progress or delete the app. The app does not request your location, contacts or photos, and it does not contain a Renaissance Labs analytics or advertising SDK.

Maps, Atlas requests and films

  • The interactive map uses Apple Maps. Apple receives network requests for the map imagery needed to display the area you browse. The app does not ask for your current location. Apple Maps & Privacy
  • Refreshing the Atlas downloads the public directory. GitHub Pages, which hosts the Atlas, logs visitor IP addresses for security. The downloaded directory may be cached on your device. GitHub Pages data collection
  • When you open a film player, the app loads the film page from Renaissance Labs Learn and an embedded YouTube player. Google receives the technical and playback information needed to provide the player, such as your IP address, device or browser information and player activity. YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed limits how a view is used for YouTube personalization. Google Privacy Policy · YouTube embedded player information

Those services explain their own retention and deletion controls in their privacy notices. You can use the bundled lab directory and lessons without opening a film or refreshing the Atlas. You can use the Atlas list without viewing the map. Links to other websites are subject to those sites’ privacy practices.

Public directory and support

The Atlas publishes organizational information and public professional names and roles, with links to the sources. We use this information to maintain the directory. If you contact us, we use your message and the contact details you provide to respond and handle your request. You can request a correction, removal, or deletion of information we hold about you through the contact channels on the Atlas About page. Ask for a private conversation before sending personal information.
