About

An atlas of independent open AI.

The Open Labs Atlas is a public directory by Renaissance Labs. It lists 227 labs across 54 countries that build AI in the open and are not owned by a big technology company. We made it because the people doing this work are scattered across Discord servers, Hugging Face orgs and small offices, and there was no single place to find them.

What counts

A lab is listed when it is independent (a nonprofit, an independent company, a community collective, an institute or a standalone academic lab), works on AI models, data, tooling or infrastructure, and has a public record of releasing open weights, open code or open datasets. We check this on its own website, Hugging Face and GitHub pages. Subsidiaries of large technology companies are left out.

Contact details and people

We only show channels the lab publishes itself: its website, Hugging Face and GitHub organisations, social accounts, and an organisation email address when the lab prints one on its own site. People are listed by name and role only when the lab or their own public profile makes that information public. We do not list personal email addresses or phone numbers.

Corrections and additions

Information changes quickly. If a lab is missing, wrong or out of date, tell us on Discord or open an issue on GitHub. Labs and people can also ask to be removed. The full dataset is available as CSV and JSON.
