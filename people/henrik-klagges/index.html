Henrik Klagges

Henrik Klagges is a co-founder of TNG Technology Consulting, founded in 2001, and chairs its supervisory board.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Klagges is first author, with Robert Dahlke, of "Assembly of Experts", which describes building the DeepSeek R1T Chimera, a hybrid of two DeepSeek mixture-of-experts models. TNG later released the three-parent R1T2 Chimera under an MIT license.
