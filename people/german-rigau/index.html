German Rigau

German Rigau is deputy director of HiTZ, the Basque Center for Language Technology in San Sebastian, and a lecturer at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), where Rigau belongs to the IXA research group.

About

Rigau previously worked in the TALP language and speech research center at the Technical University of Catalonia. Rigau's research is in natural language processing, including lexical semantics and multilingual language models.
