About

Oepen is a member of the HPLT project, was first author of the HPLT 3.0 paper describing a multilingual pre-training dataset covering close to 200 language and script combinations, and leads data work in the OpenEuroLLM project at the University of Oslo. Oepen's earlier research includes grammar engineering and semantic parsing.
