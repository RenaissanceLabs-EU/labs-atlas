Firoj Alam

Firoj Alam is a senior scientist at the Qatar Computing Research Institute (HBKU), with a PhD in computer science from the University of Trento.

About

Their research covers multilingual and low-resource language models, social media analysis and benchmarking. Alam is an author of the Fanar Arabic-centric generative AI platform report and of the LLMeBench framework.
