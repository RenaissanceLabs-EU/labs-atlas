TheBloke Handle

@TheBloke

TheBloke is a Hugging Face account, shown on its profile as Tom Jobbins, that published quantized versions of open language models in GPTQ, AWQ and GGML/GGUF formats, making many models usable on consumer hardware.

About

The account has been inactive for new model uploads since January 2024; its last upload was CapybaraHermes-2.5-Mistral-7B.

Hugging Face account with about 27,500 followers and more than 3,800 models, the main early source of quantized (GPTQ, AWQ, GGUF) community model releases.
