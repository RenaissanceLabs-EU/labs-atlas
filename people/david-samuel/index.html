David Samuel

David Samuel is a researcher in the Language Technology Group at the University of Oslo, working on language models for Norwegian and other languages.

About

Samuel is first author of NorBench, a benchmark for Norwegian language models, and co-first author of GPT-BERT, a hybrid training approach that combines masked and causal language modeling. Samuel contributes to the NorBERT model family released by the group.
