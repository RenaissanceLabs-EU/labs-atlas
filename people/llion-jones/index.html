Llion Jones

Llion Jones is a Welsh-born computer scientist and co-founder and chief technology officer of Sakana AI.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Jones studied at the University of Birmingham and worked at Google, first at YouTube and later in Google Research, before leaving in 2023. Jones is a co-author of the 2017 paper that introduced the Transformer architecture.
