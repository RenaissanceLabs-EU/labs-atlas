Mirella Lapata

Mirella Lapata is a professor of natural language processing in the School of Informatics at the University of Edinburgh and a member of its EdinburghNLP group.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Research covers semantics, summarization and generating text from multiple modalities, supported by a European Research Council grant for the TransModal project. Lapata is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh and a member of Academia Europaea.
