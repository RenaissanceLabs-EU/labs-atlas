About

Angelopoulos is a co-founder and CEO of LMArena (later branded Arena). As a Berkeley EECS PhD student, Angelopoulos launched Chatbot Arena in 2023 with Wei-Lin Chiang as a side project of the LMSYS group; it became a company in April 2025. Angelopoulos's research background includes statistics and uncertainty quantification.
