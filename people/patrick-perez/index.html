Patrick Pérez is chief executive officer of Kyutai, a non-profit open-science AI lab in Paris.

About

Before Kyutai, Pérez was vice president and scientific director for AI at the automotive supplier Valeo from 2018 to 2023. At Kyutai, Pérez co-authored the technical report on Moshi, a real-time speech-text foundation model released in 2024.
