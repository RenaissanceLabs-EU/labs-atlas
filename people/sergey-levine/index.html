Sergey Levine

Sergey Levine is a professor in EECS at UC Berkeley, which they joined in 2016, and a co-founder of Physical Intelligence.

About

Their research covers reinforcement learning, imitation learning and scalable robot learning. Levine's group developed guided policy search and end-to-end visuomotor policy training, and contributed to offline RL methods such as CQL and IQL.
