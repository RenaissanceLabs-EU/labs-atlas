Ramin Hasani

Ramin Hasani is co-founder and chief executive officer of Liquid AI, a company spun out of MIT CSAIL.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Hasani holds a PhD from TU Wien and was a postdoctoral associate at MIT CSAIL, where Hasani co-developed liquid neural networks, a class of continuous-time models inspired by the nervous system of a small worm. Liquid AI builds efficient foundation models for on-device use.
