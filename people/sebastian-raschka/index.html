About

Raschka previously worked at Lightning AI and was a statistics professor at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. Raschka writes books, a newsletter and open source code that explain how LLMs are built.

Author of Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch), Python Machine Learning and the Ahead of AI newsletter; the open source LLMs-from-scratch repository is widely used for learning how LLMs are built.
