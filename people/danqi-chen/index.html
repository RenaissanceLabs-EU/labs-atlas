Danqi Chen

Danqi Chen is an associate professor of computer science at Princeton University, co-leader of the Princeton NLP Group and associate director of Princeton Language and Intelligence.

About

Their research covers natural language processing, question answering and large language models. Chen received the 2022 Samsung AI Researcher of the Year award and an NSF CAREER Award.
