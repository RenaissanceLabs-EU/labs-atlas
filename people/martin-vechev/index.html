Martin Vechev

Martin Vechev is a professor of computer science at ETH Zurich, where Vechev heads the Secure, Reliable, and Intelligent Systems (SRI) Lab, and the founder and scientific director of INSAIT in Sofia, Bulgaria.

About

INSAIT, set up with ETH Zurich and EPFL, released the BgGPT family of Bulgarian language models. Vechev also co-founded the companies LatticeFlow, DeepCode and ChainSecurity.
