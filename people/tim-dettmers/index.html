About

They created and maintain the bitsandbytes library and work on making large models cheaper to train and run, with a current focus on agents.

Creator and maintainer of bitsandbytes and first author of QLoRA and LLM.int8(), widely used for low-memory LLM finetuning and inference; academic researcher with about 1,500 GitHub followers.
