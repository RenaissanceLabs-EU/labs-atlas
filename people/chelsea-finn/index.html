Chelsea Finn

Chelsea Finn is an assistant professor at Stanford University, where they lead the IRIS Lab, and a co-founder of Physical Intelligence.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Their research focuses on how robots can learn to learn through interaction. Finn is a co-author of Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning (MAML) and received the 2018 ACM Doctoral Dissertation Award.
