Eneko Agirre

Eneko Agirre is a full professor at the University of the Basque Country and director of HiTZ, the Basque Center for Language Technology.

About

Agirre is an ACL Fellow known for work on word sense disambiguation and semantic textual similarity. HiTZ released Latxa, an open large language model for Basque.
