Ariel Ekgren

Ariel Ekgren is a research scientist and tech lead at AI Sweden who works on large language models for Swedish and the Nordic languages.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Ekgren is the corresponding author of the GPT-SW3 papers, which describe a family of decoder-only models trained on about 320 billion tokens.
