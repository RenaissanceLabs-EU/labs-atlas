Huseyin Atakan Varol

Hüseyin Atakan Varol is the founding director of the Institute of Smart Systems and Artificial Intelligence (ISSAI) at Nazarbayev University in Kazakhstan.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Varol's background is in robotics and human-machine interfaces, and Varol has led ISSAI's work on Kazakh-language AI, including the KazLLM models delivered in December 2024.
