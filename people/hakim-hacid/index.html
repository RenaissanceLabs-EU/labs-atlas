Hakim Hacid

Hakim Hacid is Chief Researcher of the Artificial Intelligence and Digital Science Research Center at the Technology Innovation Institute (TII) in Abu Dhabi, the group behind the Falcon language models.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Hacid was appointed to the role in September 2024 after serving as acting chief researcher. Earlier, Hacid was an associate professor at Zayed University and held research roles at Bell Labs.
