Jenia Jitsev

Jenia Jitsev is a co-founder of LAION and a researcher at the Jülich Supercomputing Centre in Germany.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Jitsev is a co-author of LAION-5B, an open image-text dataset of more than five billion pairs, and of the reproducible scaling laws study for contrastive language-image learning (CVPR 2023). Jitsev's work focuses on open foundation models and scaling on supercomputers.
