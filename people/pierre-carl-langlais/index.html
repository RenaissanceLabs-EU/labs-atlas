Pierre-Carl Langlais

Pierre-Carl Langlais is a co-founder of Pleias, a French startup building language models on openly licensed data, and holds a PhD in information and communication from Sorbonne.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

They are first author of the Common Corpus paper, a roughly two trillion token collection of uncopyrighted and openly licensed text for LLM pretraining. Langlais previously was head of research at OpSci.
