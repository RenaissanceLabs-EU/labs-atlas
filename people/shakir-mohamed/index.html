Shakir Mohamed

Shakir Mohamed is a research director at Google DeepMind in London and an honorary professor of computer science at University College London.

About

Mohamed co-founded the Deep Learning Indaba, a non-profit supporting machine learning in Africa, and chairs its board of trustees. Research areas include generative models, variational inference and sociotechnical aspects of AI; Mohamed also served as general chair of ICLR 2021 and co-general chair of NeurIPS 2022.
