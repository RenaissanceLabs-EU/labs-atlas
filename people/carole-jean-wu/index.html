Carole-Jean Wu

Carole-Jean Wu is a founding member and Vice President of MLCommons and a director of AI research at Meta, where Wu leads systems and machine learning research.

About

Wu chaired the MLPerf Recommendation Benchmark Advisory Board and works on sustainable AI computing, including the ACT carbon modeling tool. Wu previously was a tenured professor at Arizona State University.
