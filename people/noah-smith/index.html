About

Smith is a professor at the University of Washington's Allen School, its inaugural vice provost for artificial intelligence, and a senior director of NLP research at Ai2. Smith works on natural language processing and machine learning, directs the OLMo open language model effort with Hannaneh Hajishirzi, and was previously on the faculty at Carnegie Mellon University.
