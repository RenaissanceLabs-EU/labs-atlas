Jesse Davis

Jesse Davis is a professor of computer science at KU Leuven in Belgium, where Davis is part of the DTAI research group and heads its Sports Analytics Lab, founded in 2014.

About

Research covers machine learning for structured data, with a focus on soccer analytics. Davis co-developed the VAEP framework for valuing player actions and co-founded the running-biomechanics spinoff runeasi.
