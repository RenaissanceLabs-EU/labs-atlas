Pelonomi Moiloa

Pelonomi Moiloa is co-founder and chief executive officer of Lelapa AI, a Johannesburg company building language technology for African languages.

On this pageAboutKnown forLabsSources

About

Lelapa's products include the Vulavula speech and text service and InkubaLM, a small multilingual language model for African languages. Moiloa was named to TIME's list of the 100 most influential people in AI.
