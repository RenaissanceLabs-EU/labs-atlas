Mauro Cettolo

Mauro Cettolo is a senior research scientist at Fondazione Bruno Kessler in Trento, where Cettolo has worked since 1992 and focused on machine translation since 2004.

About

Cettolo co-organized the IWSLT shared tasks in several years and serves as an Action Editor for Transactions of the ACL. Earlier work covered speech recognition.
