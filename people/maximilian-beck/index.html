Maximilian Beck

Maximilian Beck is a researcher at the Institute for Machine Learning at Johannes Kepler University Linz and at NXAI.

On this pageAboutKnown forLabsSources

About

Beck is first author of xLSTM: Extended Long Short-Term Memory (NeurIPS 2024) and of xLSTM 7B (ICML 2025), a recurrent language model designed for fast inference. Beck also co-authored work on efficient linear RNN kernels.
