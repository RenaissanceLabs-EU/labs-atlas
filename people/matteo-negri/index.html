Matteo Negri

Matteo Negri is a senior research scientist in the Machine Translation group at Fondazione Bruno Kessler in Trento.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Research centers on speech translation, subtitling and gender-inclusive machine translation. Negri co-authored the MuST-C corpus paper and FBK's submissions to the IWSLT shared tasks.
