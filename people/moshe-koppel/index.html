Moshe Koppel

Moshe Koppel is a professor of computer science at Bar-Ilan University and the founder of DICTA, the Israel Center for Text Analysis.

About

Koppel is known for computational authorship attribution and profiling, and for DICTA's Hebrew language tools such as the Nakdan vocalization system, described in a 2020 ACL paper, and the DictaLM models.
