Lilja Øvrelid

Lilja Øvrelid is a professor and leader of the Language Technology Group at the University of Oslo's Department of Informatics, and a co-director of the Integreat Center of Excellence.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Øvrelid's research covers syntactic and semantic processing of text with machine learning, and Øvrelid co-authored NorBench, a benchmark for Norwegian language models.
