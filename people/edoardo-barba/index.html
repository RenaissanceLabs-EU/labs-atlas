Edoardo Barba

Edoardo Barba is a researcher in the Sapienza NLP group at Sapienza University of Rome.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

They are one of the leads of Minerva, a family of Italian-English language models trained on over 500 billion words, together with Roberto Navigli and Simone Conia. Barba is also a co-author of ReLiK for entity linking and relation extraction.
