Rio Yokota

Rio Yokota is a Professor at Science Tokyo, formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology, working on high-performance computing and large-scale model training.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

Yokota leads LLM training for the Swallow project. Yokota also led the Fugaku-LLM effort, which trained a language model on the Fugaku supercomputer using a ported Megatron-DeepSpeed.
