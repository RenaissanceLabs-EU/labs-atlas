Mihai Masala

Mihai Masala is a researcher at POLITEHNICA Bucharest working on Romanian natural language processing.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

They are first author of the OpenLLM-Ro papers that introduced RoLlama, the first foundational Romanian LLMs, and the "Vorbesti Romanesti?" training recipe accepted at EMNLP 2024 Findings. All data, code and models from the recipe were released openly.
