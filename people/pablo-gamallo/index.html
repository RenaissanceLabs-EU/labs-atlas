Pablo Gamallo

Pablo Gamallo is a researcher at CiTIUS, the University of Santiago de Compostela research center, working on language technologies.

On this pageAboutKnown forActivityLabsSources

About

They are lead author of the paper introducing the first open generative LLMs for Galician, developed within the Proxecto Nos initiative. Gamallo also led the CarvalhoPT-GL model for Galician and Portuguese.
