About

Dimakis is a professor in the EECS department at UC Berkeley and co-director of the National AI Institute for Foundations of Machine Learning. They co-founded Bespoke Labs, a data curation company, and contribute to the OpenThoughts reasoning datasets. Earlier work includes compressed sensing with generative models and gradient coding.
